2. Use the price-match guarantee

EPOMAKER says it will match a lower price on the identical model from an authorized seller, before you buy or within 15 days after. If the price drops on epomaker.com within 15 days of your order, it refunds the difference when you email your order number. Coupons, bundles, clearance and pre-orders don't count as a lower price.

3. Keep standard shipping, or spend $500 for free express

Standard shipping is free to most countries and takes about 2 to 3 weeks after dispatch. Express by DHL or FedEx costs about $10 to $40 depending on the country and takes 3 to 7 days; DHL Express becomes free on orders over $500 (the TH96 and Firstblood B67 excluded).

Shipping and returns

In-stock items ship within 3 working days. Import taxes are paid by the buyer in most countries; YunExpress standard shipping includes tax for some countries.

EPOMAKER offers a 30-day return on orders in original condition with all accessories, free replacement of defective items within 30 days, and a 1-year warranty against manufacturing defects. Ask for a refund to your original payment method in your email: if you don't say within 48 hours, the refund is issued as store credit, and items bought at 50% off or more may be refunded as store credit. Customized items and Mystery Boxes can't be returned unless faulty.

EPOMAKER review: the short answer

EPOMAKER is a real keyboard brand that started on a crowdfunding platform in 2019 and scores 4.2 out of 5 from 149 reviews on Trustpilot. Buyers mostly praise the sound and build; the complaints are about battery life, patchy wireless on some models and slow standard shipping.

What EPOMAKER sells

Keyboards in many layouts: the wooden Bamboo75, the Galaxy75, the TH and RT lines, and Hall effect or TMR (magnetic switch) boards such as the HE60 and HE65 V2 TMR. It also sells switches, keycaps, mice and audio gear.

When we checked, best sellers ran from $59.99 for the HE60 and the HE65 V2 TMR (on sale from $99.99) to $109.99 for the Bamboo75.

Is EPOMAKER legit?

Yes. It has sold keyboards since a 2019 crowdfunding launch, publishes shipping, return, warranty and price-match policies, answers at support@epomaker.com, and ships with tracking to most countries. Its contact page lists a trade name and email but no street address.

Is EPOMAKER worth the money?

For a magnetic-switch or wooden keyboard under about $110, EPOMAKER is good value, and the 30-day return and 1-year warranty lower the risk. Ask for a refund to your card if you return something, because the default is store credit.
