Choose the H3 Plus for the lowest price

At $29.99, the H3 Plus costs half as much as the H8. TIDRADIO lists it at 5 watts, half the H8 and H9, but it still has the newer features: a color screen, SMS texting between radios, a spectrum view of busy and clear channels, and firmware updates from the ODmaster website, Mac included. It's also the smallest, at 112 × 53 × 32 mm.

Its Bluetooth goes further than the H8's. TIDRADIO says it connects to Bluetooth headsets, wireless PTT buttons and handheld mics, and can play music or answer calls from a paired phone. With the ODmaster app it can also work as a portable repeater or talk through the app's global intercom. It has the highest store rating of the three, from the most reviews.

The trade-off is power. Five watts reaches less far than ten in the same conditions, so if you talk across hills or long distances, look at the H8.

Choose the H8 for 10 watts at a lower price

The 4th Gen H8 gives you 10 watts for $10 less than the H9. TIDRADIO lists a 2500 mAh battery, a 240MHz processor with 128MB of storage, USB-C programming, SMS, the spectrum view, one-tap import of nearby repeaters and a portable repeater mode. It also has a flashlight and an emergency alert. You can program it from the ODmaster phone app over Bluetooth, from a web browser or with CPS software on a computer.

TIDRADIO's own FAQ suggests the H8 or H3 for people who have just got their license, because accessories are easy to find and programming instructions are simple. The 4th Gen page has only 13 store ratings so far, averaging 4.15 out of 5, so there's less buyer feedback to go on than for the other two.

Choose the H9 for APRS and GPS

The H9 is the only one of the three with GPS-based APRS. That lets you share your live position with other APRS users and see it on a map such as aprs.fi, which matters for hiking groups, search teams and events. It also has a Morse code emergency mode, two customizable PTT buttons and Bluetooth 5.1 for headsets, PTT buttons and mics.

Two things to know first. TIDRADIO's FAQ says the H9 is not compatible with CHIRP programming software for now, so you'd program it with ODmaster instead. It also says the GPS usually gets your altitude only outdoors in the open. If you don't need location sharing, the H8 has the same 10 watts for less.

Before you buy

In the US, transmitting on ham frequencies needs an amateur license, and GMRS needs its own FCC license. Listening to the airband, FM radio and NOAA weather channels doesn't. Rules differ in other countries; TIDRADIO's own page notes that the Unlocked version doesn't meet German rules and points buyers there to the Ham version.

TIDRADIO says it ships within 1 to 3 days, and its site shows a 1-year warranty. You have 12 days from delivery to ask for an exchange or refund, the item must be new in its original packaging, and you pay the return shipping.

Questions people ask

Which TIDRADIO is best?

It depends on what you need. The H3 Plus is the cheapest at $29.99 and has the highest store rating. The 4th Gen H8 gives you 10 watts for $59.99. The H9, at $69.99, is the one with APRS and GPS.

What's the difference between the TIDRADIO H3 and H3 Plus?

TIDRADIO's comparison table gives the H3 Plus Bluetooth for headsets, PTT buttons and mics, SMS texting and the spectrum view, with 1MB and 128MB of storage. The older H3 has Bluetooth programming only and none of those features.

TIDRADIO H8 vs H9: which should I get?

Both are listed at 10 watts. Get the H9 if you need APRS location sharing or Bluetooth headsets. Otherwise the H8 costs $10 less and is the one TIDRADIO suggests for beginners.

Is the TIDRADIO H3 Plus 5 watts or 10 watts?

TIDRADIO's product page lists the H3 Plus at up to 5 watts. The H8 and H9 are the 10-watt models.

Does the TIDRADIO H8 have Bluetooth?

The 4th Gen H8 can be programmed from the ODmaster phone app over Bluetooth. Its feature list doesn't mention Bluetooth headsets or PTT buttons; TIDRADIO lists those for the H3 Plus and H9.

Does the TIDRADIO H9 work with CHIRP?

Not for now. TIDRADIO's FAQ says the H9 isn't compatible with CHIRP yet. You can program it with the ODmaster app or website.

Can TIDRADIO radios use GMRS?

Yes. The H3 Plus, H8 and H9 each come in a GMRS version at the same price as the Ham and Unlocked versions. In the US you need a GMRS license to transmit.

Can two TIDRADIO radios text each other?

Yes. TIDRADIO lists SMS texting on the H3 Plus, the 4th Gen H8 and the H9, between radios on the same frequency.
