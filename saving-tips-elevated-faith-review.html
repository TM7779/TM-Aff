The short answer

Yes, Elevated Faith is legit. It's a US brand based in Oshkosh, Wisconsin, founded in 2015 by Josh Gander, and its best sellers average 4.8 to 4.9 stars from hundreds of reviews each. Trustpilot gives it 4.6 from 408 reviews. The main complaints are slow or missing deliveries and slow replies from customer service.

What Elevated Faith sells

Faith-themed jewelry and clothing: WWJD and verse bracelets, rings, necklaces, hoodies, tees, crewnecks, phone cases, stickers and kids' clothes. Elevated Faith says it gives a portion of every sale to faith-based organizations.

Best sellers include the WWJD Bracelet at $4.99, the Highs and Lows Ring at $9.99, the Promise Ring at $19.99 and the Child of God Unisex Hoodie at $69.99.
