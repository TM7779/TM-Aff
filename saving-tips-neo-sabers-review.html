The short answer

NEO Sabers is a real US lightsaber store with a Berkeley address and phone number, and it scores 4.0 out of 5 from 518 reviews on Trustpilot. Most buyers are happy with the sabers; the complaints are about delays, packaging and support during busy periods, and change-of-mind returns cost a $20 fee plus postage.

What NEO Sabers sells

Dueling and display lightsabers with a choice of sound board (XRGB 3.0, Xenopixel V3 or NeoProffie), replica hilts such as the OWK Replica II, legendary designs like the Bastila and Executioner, double-bladed sabers, belt clips, wall art and wedding bundles.

When we checked, sabers ran from $179 for the OWK Replica II with XRGB to $459 and more for Xenopixel and Proffie models.

Recent five-star reviews praise the build and look of the sabers, and support staff who sorted out a courier mix-up. The one-star reviews describe shipping much later than promised, replies that felt automated, a saber with a screw too tight to remove, and an order that never came. One four-star buyer liked the saber but not the thin packaging. NEO Sabers has claimed its Trustpilot page. We didn't find star ratings on the product pages.

Is NEO Sabers legit?

Yes. Its store lists NEOSabers at 1332 4th Street, Berkeley, California, with a phone number and support email, and publishes shipping, return and warranty terms. Orders ship with tracking, and the store tests each saber before dispatch.

Is it worth the money?

For a first pixel saber, the XRGB versions are the better value: the OWK Replica II was $179 against $239 with Xenopixel, and it ships sooner. Buy only what you're sure of, since returns cost a $20 fee, return postage and payment fees.
