The short answer

Yes, BabyQuip is legit. It's a US company founded in 2016 that says it has handled more than 500,000 reservations in over 5,000 cities. The main thing to know: your gear comes from an independent local provider, so how good your rental is depends a lot on the provider you pick.

What BabyQuip rents

Baby and kids' gear for trips: full-size cribs with a mattress and sheets, playards, bassinets, strollers, car seats, high chairs, toys, baby monitors and bath gear. It also rents beach gear, pet gear and mobility aids like wheelchairs, and some providers sell diapers and groceries.

Rates are set by each provider. In Orlando, the lowest daily rates were $14 for a full-size crib, $6 for a playard, $5 for a single stroller, $8 for a convertible car seat and $4 for a high chair, before delivery, BabyQuip's 6 to 9% service fee and taxes.

How it works

You enter your destination and dates, choose gear and a provider, and pay in full when you book. The provider, called a Quality Provider, contacts you to arrange delivery to your hotel, vacation rental, family's home or the airport, sets the gear up (except car seats and baby-proofing items) and picks it up at the end. Some providers also let you collect and return the gear yourself.

BabyQuip says it has about 3,000 providers. They own their gear, and BabyQuip says they pass background checks and complete training on safety and cleaning before they start. Every rental includes liability insurance, and in the US a damage protection plan covers accidental damage up to $1,000 after a $25 charge.

Is BabyQuip legit?

Yes. It's an established company with a public team page, a help center, a customer support team, an app on the App Store and clear written rules for fees, damage and cancellations. You pay through BabyQuip, not the provider, so the cancellation and refund rules apply whichever provider you book.

To lower the risk of a bad rental, pick a provider with many completed deliveries and good reviews on their profile, book early for busy dates like spring break and the holidays, and message the provider before the trip to confirm the gear and the delivery time.

Is it worth the money?

For a week-long trip, a crib, stroller and car seat from the lowest Orlando rates come to $189 before delivery and fees, so it's not cheap. What you get is a full-size crib with a real mattress instead of a travel cot, no bulky gear to carry through the airport, and setup before you arrive. For short trips, or if your own travel gear works, bringing it may cost less.

Questions people ask

Is BabyQuip legit?

Yes. It's a US company founded in 2016, with more than 500,000 reservations by its own count, liability insurance on every rental and written rules for fees and refunds. Your experience depends on the local provider you choose.

Is BabyQuip safe?

BabyQuip says its providers pass background checks, train on safety and cleaning, clean gear after every rental and watch for recalls. Every rental includes liability insurance. Providers can't install car seats, so follow the manual or ask a certified technician.

What are BabyQuip's reviews like?

On its own site, BabyQuip says 99% of about 100,000 reviews are five stars. Its app rates 4.0 out of 5 from 56 ratings on the App Store, and Trustpilot has only two reviews, both 1 star, checked on November 11, 2026.

What do BabyQuip complaints say?

The complaints we found are about providers: one didn't deliver, and in another case a reservation was moved to a different provider a week before the trip. Picking a provider with many good reviews lowers the risk.

How does BabyQuip work?

Enter your destination and dates, pick gear and a local provider and pay. The provider delivers, sets up and picks up the gear, or you collect it yourself if they offer self-pickup.

How much does BabyQuip cost?

Rates depend on the provider. In Orlando they started at $14 a day for a crib, $5 for a stroller and $8 for a car seat, plus delivery, a 6 to 9% service fee and taxes.

Are BabyQuip cribs clean?

BabyQuip requires providers to clean and inspect gear after every pickup and again before the next delivery, and each crib comes with a mattress, mattress cover and fitted sheet. BabyQuip says 96.8% of customers felt their gear was clean.

Where is BabyQuip available?

In more than 5,000 cities across the US, Canada, Mexico, the Caribbean, Australia, New Zealand and parts of Europe, according to BabyQuip.

What happens if I damage BabyQuip gear?

In the US, if the provider files a claim, you pay a $25 minor damage charge and BabyQuip covers accidental damage up to $1,000. Heavily soiled gear can cost a $25 cleaning fee per item.

Can I cancel a BabyQuip reservation?

Yes, from your account. Cancel 15 days or more before the start for a refund of rental fees, minus the service fee. Closer to the start, rental fees aren't refunded unless you added Cancel For Any Reason.
