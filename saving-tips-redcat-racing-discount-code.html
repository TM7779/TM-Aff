Redcat Rewards is free and open to US customers only. You get 250 points for signing up and 5 points per $1 spent, rising to 7 points after $1,000 and 10 points after $3,000 in total spending. Points are worth $1 per 200 at checkout, or $5 per 1,000 as a voucher.

Shipping and returns

Shipping is calculated at checkout; we didn't see a free-shipping threshold on redcatracing.com when we checked. Checkout also offers Affirm monthly payments, which spread the cost but don't lower it.

Returns are accepted within 30 days of purchase for items that are unused, complete and in the original packaging, with a receipt or proof of purchase. Start a return by emailing Redcat's support address for an RMA. You pay the return shipping, and shipping costs aren't refunded. Exchanges are only for defective or damaged items. Vehicles carry a one-year limited warranty, and claims go through Redcat, not the hobby shop you bought from.

Redcat Racing review: the short answer

Redcat Racing is a real RC car company based in Phoenix, Arizona. Its own giveaway page says Redcat turned 20 on May 5, 2025, and the company behind it is Namero LLC, doing business as Redcat Racing. Vehicles come ready to run with a radio, battery and charger, prices start around $120, and they carry a one-year limited warranty. Returns are 30 days, for unused items only, and you pay the return shipping.

What Redcat Racing sells

Rock crawlers in 1/18 and 1/10 scale (Ascent-18, Everest, Ascent Fusion), monster trucks (Volcano-16, Volcano EPX), trail trucks such as the Gen 9 International Harvester Scout 800A, and hopping lowriders with officially licensed Chevrolet bodies (SixtyFour, SixtyFour-18, Monte Carlo). The store also sells batteries, chargers, replacement parts and hop-up upgrades for its vehicles, with user manuals and parts lists to download.

When we checked, the Volcano-16 cost $119.95, the Ascent-18 $129.99, the Everest Gen7 Sport $229.95, the Monte Carlo $419.99 and the 1/10 SixtyFour $549.99.
