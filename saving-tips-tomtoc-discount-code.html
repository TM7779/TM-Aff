Shipping and returns

Orders are usually processed within 24 to 48 hours on weekdays and shipped in 1 to 3 business days. tomtoc estimates 3 to 7 business days for delivery and says shipping isn't yet available to some countries, so check yours at checkout. Undamaged items in original condition with tags can be returned within 30 days for a full refund. If you return an item that came with a free gift, tomtoc may deduct the gift's list price.
