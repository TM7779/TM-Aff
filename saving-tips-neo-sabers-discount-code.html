1. Pick the sound board you need

Most sabers come with a choice of sound board, and it changes the price more than the code does. XRGB sabers also ship faster (about 5 days against 10 to 15 for Xenopixel and Proffie).

2. Look at the Buy 1 Get 2 Free offer

When we checked, the store banner offered Buy 1 Get 2 Free on replica pixel sabers for a limited time. If you want sabers for more than one person, compare that offer with the code on a single saber at checkout.

3. Order early for a gift

NEO Sabers asks for 4 to 5 days of processing during the sale season, as each saber is tested before it ships, and warns of delays of 1 to 2 weeks when orders pile up. Paying for a faster board won't help if the order leaves late, so order a few weeks ahead.

Shipping and returns

Shipping is free on US orders over $99 during the sale season. International buyers pay customs charges.

You can return a saber you don't like within 30 days in original condition, paying return shipping and a $20 processing fee; shipping costs and card or PayPal fees are also deducted from the refund. Defective sabers reported with photos and video within 30 days get replacement parts, and wrong items are refunded or resent at the store's cost. Product pages also mention a 1-year warranty.
