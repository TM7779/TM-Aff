What's in Genuine Essiac

Eight powdered herbs: blessed thistle, burdock root, kelp, red clover, sheep sorrel (including the root), slippery elm bark, Turkish rhubarb root and watercress. Because the herbs are powdered, they stay in the brew.

Essiac is a traditional herbal tea, not a treatment for any illness. If you have a health condition, are pregnant or take medication, talk to your doctor before drinking it.
