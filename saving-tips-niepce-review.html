The short answer

Niepce is a real US store that has sold streetwear online since 2018, with free US shipping and a 14-day return window. Its best sellers rate well on its own site, but its few Trustpilot reviews are mixed, with complaints about quality and shipping. Start with one item, check the size chart and keep the return window in mind.

What Niepce sells

Unisex streetwear and techwear: strapped and cargo joggers, lightweight pants, embroidered hoodies and zip hoodies, windbreakers, tees, sneakers and crossbody bags.

Best sellers include the YE2032 Matte Black Joggers at $39, the HiBag2 Industrial Crossbody Bag at $45 and the YC135 Pink Flowers Lightweight Pants at $19. A Clearance section lists items at 50 to 80% off.

On Niepce's own site, its best sellers average between 4.2 and 4.8 stars. Its Trustpilot page isn't managed by the company and has only 12 reviews, most of them from 2019 to 2021.

Happy buyers mention comfortable clothes, good quality and fast US delivery. Unhappy ones mention lower quality than expected, slow tracking and an order held for a fraud check that asked for ID. Two older reviews say the clothes were dropshipped or came from China, and a 2025 review complains that a T-shirt print looked AI-generated.

Is Niepce legit?

Niepce is a real business: its store is registered in California, its About page says it started in 2018 and is based in Los Angeles, and it sells through its own site with a written return policy. Some items can also be bought with Buy with Prime.

Is it worth the money?

Most best-selling pants cost $19 to $48 and an embroidered hoodie $68, with 50 to 80% off in Clearance, and US shipping is free. Quality reports vary, so the 14-day return window and prepaid return label matter: try things on as soon as they arrive.
