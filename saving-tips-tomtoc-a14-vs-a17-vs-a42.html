Choose the A14 for a slim, snug fit

The A14 is the smallest and cheapest of the three. Its outside is about half an inch narrower than the A17 and A42, and its laptop compartment is shallower, 0.63 inches against 0.91, so a 14-inch MacBook Pro sits closer to the padding with less room to slide around. That also means less room for a thick laptop case.

It has one wide front pocket instead of two, at 12.8 × 7.48 inches, with slots inside for a pen, a cable and a charger. That suits people who carry a few flat things: a charger, a notebook, a cable. tomtoc describes it as a "slim tri-compartment design" with multiple carrying options, but its listing doesn't spell out a shoulder strap, so if you need one, check the photos on the product page or pick the A42.

The A14 also has the most buyer feedback, 301 ratings averaging 4.8, and when we checked, tomtoc's site was showing it at 10% off in the cart, $36.90 in black. That's almost exactly what the code gives, so the code may not take more off this bag. Compare the totals at checkout.

Choose the A17 for more pocket space

The A17 is the one to get if your pockets are full. Its two front pockets split chargers and a mouse from cables, pens and cards, and its back pocket is the biggest of the three at 13.7 × 8.07 inches, enough for papers or a thin notebook. tomtoc says it's made from spill-resistant recycled fabric with a water-repellent finish without added PFAS.

You carry it by its padded PU leather handles. tomtoc's listing doesn't mention a shoulder strap, so it's built for walking from car to office or around an airport, not for a long commute on foot. It has the highest store rating, 4.9 from 93 ratings, and costs $2 more than the A14 in black.

Choose the A42 if you want a shoulder strap

On paper, the A42 is the A17 with a strap. tomtoc lists the same outside size, the same laptop space and the same three pockets, down to the inch. What you get for the extra $3 is a detachable shoulder strap, which tomtoc describes for "commuting and business travel".

The 14-inch A42 has only 6 ratings on tomtoc's site so far, averaging 4.7, so there's less feedback than for the A14 and A17. Because the bag body matches the A17, the A17's reviews tell you a fair amount about the pockets and fit, though not about the strap.
