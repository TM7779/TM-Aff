1. Match the part number before you order

Every JLM Marine product page lists the OEM numbers it replaces, and the store has a parts finder by make, horsepower, stroke, model, year and serial number. JLM Marine says its parts are aftermarket replacements, not dealer OEM parts. Checking the fit first matters, because a change-of-mind return costs a 20% restocking fee and the return shipping.

2. Shipping is already free

Standard shipping is free on all orders with no minimum, so you don't need to add parts to reach a threshold. Faster shipping can be chosen at checkout, depending on where you live.

3. Order a full kit instead of single parts

The impeller repair kits come with the housing, gaskets and hardware for a full water pump rebuild, and some carburetor kits are sold in sets of three for multi-carb engines. With a full kit, one order and one use of the code covers the whole job.

Shipping and returns

JLM Marine ships worldwide, with some product and destination limits. For the US and Canada, it says orders from Amazon FBA stock arrive in about 1–5 business days, from its US warehouse in about 7 days, and from its China warehouse in about 12 days. Import taxes and duties aren't included in the price.

Unused items in their original packaging can be returned within 7 days of delivery (14 days in the EU) after you get a return number from JLM Marine. Change-of-mind returns have a 20% restocking fee and the original shipping isn't refunded; wrong, damaged or faulty parts are replaced or refunded with no fee. Many parts carry a 1-year warranty against manufacturing defects.
