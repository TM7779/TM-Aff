1. Pick by brightness, not only by price

VANKYO's models range from 150 to 730 lumens on its own spec sheets, and brightness decides how dark the room has to be. The $69.99 Leisure 3W Flip and the $119.99 Leisure 3R Air are 720p and 150 lumens, made for small rooms and dark bedrooms. The H500R and V700 Neo are native 1080p at 330 and 400 ANSI lumens. The V700G Pro is the brightest at 730 ANSI lumens, which the brand pitches for indoor and outdoor use.

Paying $20 to $30 less for a dimmer model is no saving if you end up watching with the lights off every time, so match the lumens to your room first, then apply JAMESWICKENDEN.

Shipping and returns

VANKYO's shipping policy (updated July 29, 2026) gives free standard shipping on eligible ivankyo.com orders to the contiguous United States. It doesn't ship to Alaska, Hawaii, Puerto Rico, US territories or APO, FPO and DPO addresses. Most in-stock orders arrive within 3 to 7 business days, and orders aren't usually processed or shipped on weekends or holidays.

Eligible products can be returned within 30 calendar days of delivery, including for a change of mind, but you must contact customer support first and the return has to include all accessories and the original packaging where possible. If VANKYO confirms a defect, damage or a wrong item, it covers reasonable return shipping. For change-of-mind returns you pay return shipping, and original shipping charges are generally not refunded. Projectors carry a one-year warranty from the purchase date. Products bought on Amazon, Walmart or other sellers go back through that seller.
