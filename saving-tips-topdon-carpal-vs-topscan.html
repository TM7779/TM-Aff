Choose CarPal for check-ups and common resets

CarPal is TOPDON's tool for everyday drivers. It scans all the car's systems, sorts fault codes into severe and minor, gives an overall health score, and pairs each code with repair instructions. It also shows live data as graphs or gauges, logs battery voltage during start-up, and can turn your phone into a heads-up display on the windshield.

Its 6 resets are the ones most people need after routine work: oil service, tire pressure (TPMS), electronic parking brake, battery (BMS), throttle adaptation and DPF regeneration on diesels. TOPDON says it covers 98% of models made from 1996 to 2023, and it can be bound to up to 3 car brands at once, which suits a household with two or three cars.

What TOPDON's CarPal page doesn't list is active tests, where the tool tells a part to switch on so you can see if it works. If you want that, step up to a TopScan.

Choose TopScan Lite for active tests on a budget

Every TopScan is bi-directional: it can send commands to fuel injectors, windows, mirrors, lights, EGR valves and more, so you can check a part without taking it off. TOPDON lists full-system diagnostics on more than 120 car brands, gateway unlocks for FCA, Renault, Nissan and others (some need an OEM account), AutoVIN, a repair data library and its TopFix repair assistant.

The Lite has 8 service functions. At €65.99 without VAT it's €25 more than CarPal, which buys you the active tests and wider coverage. The adapter is small, 84 × 50 × 27 mm and 78 g, and talks to the phone by Bluetooth 5.0.

Choose TopScan Pro for more resets and IMMO

TOPDON's support page sums up the Pro as the Lite plus window calibration, injector coding, TPMS reset, sunroof reset and seat calibration, and immobilizer (IMMO) functions, 13 service functions in all. It costs €109.00 without VAT, and its update renewal after the first year is $99 against $49.99 for the Lite.

On upgrading later, TOPDON's support page contradicts itself: one line says a Lite can't be upgraded to a Pro, another prices the upgrade at $99. If you think you'll need Pro functions, buying the Pro at the start is the safer choice.

Choose TopScan Master for newer cars and 30 functions

The Master has 30 service functions, including the likes of injector coding and gearbox matching, and is the only TopScan with DoIP, the protocol many newer cars use. TOPDON names models from GM, Chrysler, Mercedes-Benz, BMW, Volvo and Land Rover. At €175.99 without VAT it's for people who work on newer cars or several makes.

Before you buy

  • Check your car first. TOPDON has a vehicle coverage search on its site and offers to confirm a special function for your car's year and model within 24 hours. Most bad reviews of TOPDON tools come from a car or function that wasn't supported.
  • Expect to go online. TOPDON says newer models keep their diagnostic data online, so some scans need an internet connection, and the tool must be activated in the app.
  • Check your phone. TopScan needs Android 7.0 or iOS 11.0 or later and a 64-bit phone.
  • Mind the return fees. TOPDON Europe takes no service fee on an unopened tool returned within 14 days, but 10% to 20% on an opened one, plus return shipping.

Questions people ask

What's the difference between the TOPDON CarPal and TopScan?

CarPal is the simpler tool: an all-system check-up, a health score and 6 service resets. TopScan adds bi-directional active tests and, depending on the edition, 8, 13 or 30 service functions. In Europe CarPal is €40.99 and TopScan starts at €65.99, both without VAT.

TopScan Lite vs Pro: what's the difference?

TOPDON says the Pro adds window calibration, injector coding, TPMS reset, sunroof reset and seat calibration, plus immobilizer (IMMO) functions. It costs €109.00 against €65.99 for the Lite, without VAT.

Is the TopScan Master worth it over the Pro?

The Master lists 30 service functions against 13 on the Pro, and DoIP support for newer cars from brands such as BMW, Mercedes-Benz, Volvo and Land Rover. If your car needs DoIP or a function only the Master lists, it's the one to get; otherwise the Pro covers most home jobs.

Can I upgrade TopScan Lite to Pro?

TOPDON's support page gives two answers: one line says an upgrade isn't available, another lists it at $99 with a year of updates. Ask TOPDON before you buy the Lite if you might want the Pro later.

Does TopScan need a subscription?

Each TopScan includes one year of free updates. After that, TOPDON says basic functions such as full-system code reading, engine inspection and I/M readiness keep working; renewing keeps everything else. Its support page lists $49.99 a year for the Lite and $99 for the Pro.

Is TOPDON CarPal bi-directional?

TOPDON's CarPal page lists check-ups, live data, a battery test and 6 resets, but not active (bi-directional) tests. Those are a TopScan feature.

What resets does CarPal do?

Oil service, TPMS, electronic parking brake, battery (BMS), throttle adaptation and DPF regeneration.

How many cars can CarPal be used on?

TOPDON says CarPal can be bound to up to 3 car brands at once, and supports 98% of models made from 1996 to 2023.

Does TopScan work on iPhone?

Yes. TOPDON lists iOS 11.0 or later and Android 7.0 or later. On iPhone the app is called TopGuru; the phone needs a 64-bit processor.
