The short answer

BodyBio is a legitimate US brand with a well-rated main product and an unusually long 60-day return window. It's premium priced, so buy the bigger bottles or subscribe, and check your subscription settings.

What BodyBio sells

Phospholipid and cell-health supplements: BodyBio PC (phospholipid complex), Butyrate, the E-Lyte electrolyte concentrate, Balance Oil and kits.

BodyBio PC is $60.99 for 60 softgels, $85.99 for 100 and $199.99 for 300.

Is BodyBio legit?

Yes. BodyBio is a US company that ships most orders from Southern California, usually within 24 hours on weekdays, and accepts return requests within 60 days of purchase.

Is it worth the money?

PC costs about $1.02 a softgel in the 60-count bottle but about $0.67 in the 300-count. Subscribe & Save takes 15% off, the same as the code, so buying big and regularly is the way to make it reasonable.
