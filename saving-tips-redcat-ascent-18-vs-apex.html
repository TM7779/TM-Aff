Choose the Ascent-18 for a first crawler

The standard Ascent-18 is Redcat's entry into 1/18 scale crawling, and at $129.99 it's half the price of the APEX. Redcat says it brings the features of its 1/10 crawlers down to a crawler that fits in your hand: ball bearings instead of bushings, portal axles, metal ring and pinion gears, machined aluminum spools, aluminum-capped oil-filled shocks and a metal gear micro servo.

The current version uses High Steer geometry, with a redesigned front axle housing and portals for more steering lock and more clearance at the C-hub. The vintage-style body is hinged at the front, so it tilts up for quick access to the electronics, and it comes in four colors.

It runs on a brushed 180-size motor and a 7.4V 750mAh 2S LiPo, charged by USB. Redcat sells a full line of hop-up parts for it, so the motor, wheels and other parts can be upgraded later.

Choose the APEX for brushless power out of the box

Redcat describes the Ascent-18 APEX as the original with over 80% of the platform reworked. The biggest change is the power system: a HobbyWing HW-1621 3500KV brushless outrunner and a HobbyWing WP-Mini24 speed control that takes 2S or 3S LiPo. The chassis is carbon fiber, built low for a lower center of gravity, with the motor moved forward and an angled skid plate.

The APEX also gets wider High Steer portal axles for a tighter turning radius, overdrive gears, a changed ring and pinion ratio, and brass parts at the front outer portal boxes and differential cover to add weight low down. It rolls on 1.3-inch beadlock wheels with crawler tires, and the body is a two-piece cab with an optional bed. Redcat says it works with most Ascent-18 hop-up parts.

The battery is the one spec to read carefully: Redcat's page lists a 3S 11.4V 200mAh LiPo. Check the manual on the product page before you buy spares.

Before you buy

  • Buy AA batteries. Both radios need 4 AA batteries, which aren't in the box.
  • Price the upgrades. If you'd fit brushless power and beadlocks to a standard Ascent-18 anyway, add up those parts first; the APEX may cost less than the upgraded standard model.
  • Decide before you open it. Redcat takes returns within 30 days only for unused items in the original packaging, and you pay the return shipping. Both carry a one-year limited warranty, handled by Redcat.

Questions people ask

What's the difference between the Redcat Ascent-18 and the Ascent-18 APEX?

The standard Ascent-18 has a brushed 180-size motor, aluminum chassis rails and a 2S LiPo battery, and costs $129.99. The APEX has a HobbyWing brushless motor and ESC, a carbon fiber low center of gravity chassis, wider High Steer portal axles, brass weights and 1.3-inch beadlock wheels, and costs $249.99. Redcat says over 80% of the platform was reworked for the APEX.

Is the Redcat Ascent-18 brushless?

The standard Ascent-18 is brushed: Redcat lists an RC180 brushed motor and a 2-in-1 brushed ESC and receiver. The brushless model is the Ascent-18 APEX, with a HobbyWing HW-1621 3500KV outrunner motor and a WP-Mini24 ESC.

Is the Ascent-18 APEX worth the extra money?

It costs $120 more, $249.99 against $129.99. You get a brushless system, a carbon fiber chassis, beadlock wheels and brass weights already fitted. If you plan to upgrade a standard Ascent-18 with similar parts anyway, the APEX saves you the work; for a first crawler, the standard model costs half as much.

What battery does the Redcat Ascent-18 use?

A 7.4V 750mAh 2S LiPo, included with a USB charger. The radio needs 4 AA batteries, which aren't included.

What battery does the Ascent-18 APEX use?

Redcat's product page lists a 3S 11.4V 200mAh LiPo with a USB charger, included. The ESC accepts 2S or 3S LiPo. The radio needs 4 AA batteries.

Do Ascent-18 upgrades fit the APEX?

Redcat says the APEX is compatible with most Ascent-18 hop-up parts. Check the hop-up list on the APEX product page for each part.

How big is the Redcat Ascent-18?

Redcat lists the Ascent-18 at 218 mm long, 130 mm wide and 114 mm high, with a 155 mm wheelbase. The APEX is 222 by 143 by 98 mm, with a 156 mm wheelbase.

What colors do the Ascent-18 and APEX come in?

The standard Ascent-18 comes in mint green, pink, blue and red. The APEX comes in red or gray, with a two-piece cab body and an optional bed.
