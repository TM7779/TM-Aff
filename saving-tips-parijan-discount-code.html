1. Reach €100 for free shipping

Shipping is €7.95 on orders under €100 and free above it, to every country. Two sweaters and a pair of trousers (€119.85 before the code) clear the bar; one sweater doesn't. PARIJAN says it covers customs fees.

2. Buy the 5-pack if you want several sweaters

The TAEBAEK sweater 5-pack was €159.95, about €31.99 each, against €39.95 for one. That's cheaper per sweater than one sweater with the code.

3. Check the return rules before a big sale order

Most of the store was marked down (up to 70% off, says its Early Black Friday banner). Its policy page says returns aren't accepted on sale items, while the return page says 30 days for unworn items with tags. Check the size chart and ask support before you order a lot.

Shipping and returns

Orders are handled in 1 to 3 business days. Delivery takes 6 to 10 business days to Europe and the United States, and 10 to 14 business days elsewhere, with a tracking number by email.

Returns must be authorized first by emailing info@parijanofficial.com, within 30 days of delivery, unworn, with tags and original packaging. Exchanges are for faulty or wrong items only, not for a different size or color. Refunds go back to your payment method in about 5 business days after the return is approved.
