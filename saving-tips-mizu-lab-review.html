The short answer

Mizu Lab is a small US brand with very good early reviews and unusual tastes. It has fewer reviews than big brands, and it doesn't take returns, so start with a variety pack.

What Mizu Lab sells

Zero-sugar electrolyte mixes in 15-stick pouches, each stick with over 1,000mg of electrolytes, in tastes like Japanese Soda, White Peach Candy and Yuzu & Mango. It also sells tumblers.

A single-taste pouch is $24.99, about $1.67 a stick; variety packs are $27.99.

Is Mizu Lab legit?

Yes, it's a real US brand selling through its own store, with free shipping over $60. It doesn't accept returns because the mixes are consumable, but asks you to contact it if something is wrong with an order.

Is it worth the money?

At about $1.67 a stick, or $1.50 with the code or a subscription, it's a treat compared with plain water, but fair if the tastes keep you drinking. Three pouches clear the free-shipping line.
