The short answer

PARIJAN is a real menswear store with a Paris address, and its clothes are cheap during its frequent sales. But it scores only 1.7 out of 5 from 694 reviews on Trustpilot, mostly over late deliveries and slow refunds, so order only what you'd be happy to wait for and keep.

What PARIJAN sells

Men's sweaters and cardigans, linen shirts and pants, knitted polos, trousers, jackets, loungewear sets, shoes, sunglasses and full outfits, in a quiet, old money look.

When we checked, almost everything was on sale: the TAEBAEK zipped cable sweater at €39.95 (was €89.95) and the PORTOFINO linen shirt at €48.95 (was €141.95).

The two scores tell different stories. The store's own badge comes from reviews it collects; Trustpilot is open to anyone. Recent one-star reviews there describe orders placed in July that arrived in September or October, parts of an order arriving separately, refunds still waiting after a return, and one buyer who says nothing arrived. The positive reviews praise the style, the sale prices and quick replies from support. PARIJAN has claimed its Trustpilot page.

Is PARIJAN legit?

It's a real business: the store lists an address at 2 Rue de Sfax, Paris, an email for support, written shipping and return policies, and tracked delivery. Its About page says it was founded by Thomas and Julien. The low Trustpilot score is about service, not about whether it exists, but it's a real risk to weigh.

Is it worth the money?

On sale, the prices are low for the look. The catch is the service record, the no size-exchange rule and a policy page that excludes sale items from returns. Pay by card or PayPal, keep your order emails, and check the size chart first.
