The short answer

Jessup is a real makeup brush brand founded in 2013 and based in Shenzhen, China, and it's widely sold online. Its brush sets cost $20 to $50, shipping is free to 50+ countries, and returns are allowed for 90 days. There's little about the store on Trustpilot.

What Jessup Beauty sells

Makeup brush sets (face, eye and complete sets of up to 25 brushes), single professional brushes, makeup sponges, brush cleaners, storage, false eyelashes and mixing tools.

When we checked, sets ran from $19.99 for a 3-piece set to $49.99 for the 25-piece T175.
