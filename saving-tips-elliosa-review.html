The short answer

Elliosa is a legitimate UK brand with strong reviews for grip and comfort. Single pairs are pricey; buy a bundle to bring the cost down, and allow a week or so for delivery outside the UK.

What Elliosa sells

Grip socks for Pilates, yoga and barre, made mostly from organic cotton (85% in the Pilates & Lattes design), plus caps, bags and bundles.

Single pairs are $18 to $24; the Grip Basics 6-pack is $72, $12 a pair.

Of 352 Trustpilot reviews, 295 are five stars. Reviewers mention how grippy the socks are and helpful customer service; a few mention delivery problems.

Is Elliosa legit?

Yes. Elliosa is a UK-based company with a claimed Trustpilot profile. Orders ship in 1 to 3 business days and take 4 to 8 business days to the US. Unused socks can be returned within 30 days; outside the UK you pay the return postage.

Is it worth the money?

A single pair at $18 to $24 is more than basic grip socks, but bundles bring it to about $12 a pair. If you go to classes weekly, a bundle is the better buy.
