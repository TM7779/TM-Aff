Shipping and returns

Orders are processed within 1 to 2 business days. Parfait ships to the US (Hawaii and Alaska included, from $12.99), Canada, the UK and the EU; outside the US the cost depends on the order and destination, and import duties aren't refunded.

Unworn, unwashed items can be returned within 30 days of the order date, or 60 days if you add REDO coverage at checkout, through the returns portal on its site. US return labels are free, but the original shipping isn't refunded. Canadian returns cost CAD $14.95, and UK and EU buyers pay the return postage. Final Sale items can't be returned, and a free gift that came with the order must go back too. Our Parfait review sums up the ratings and the complaints.
