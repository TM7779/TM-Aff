The short answer

Parfait is a real, long-running brand with a Shopify store, published shipping and return policies and a returns portal. Its best-known strapless bra, the Elissa, rates 4.0 out of 5 from 119 reviews on its own store. The complaints that repeat are about fit: some buyers found a style ran large or didn't support a heavy bust. Use the size chart, and know the return rules before you order outside the US.

What Parfait sells

Bras first: underwire, wire-free, T-shirt, plunge, minimizer, longline, strapless, nursing and sports bras, most in band sizes 28 or 30 to 42 or 44 and cups from US C or D up to K or L, depending on the style. It also sells bralettes, panties (single and in 3 or 6 packs), bodysuits, chemises and bustiers. Best sellers when we checked included the Elise T-shirt bra ($58), the wire-free Simplicity bra ($69, sold in S to 3X Plus) and the Elissa longline strapless bra ($66).

Its About page says the company was founded in 2005, is family-owned and has offices in the UK, Canada, Hong Kong and India besides its Los Angeles headquarters. Those are the brand's own words.

The Elissa has enough reviews to judge: 76 of 119 give five stars, 11 four, 9 three, 7 two and 16 one. The five-star reviews come from buyers with fuller busts, some in sizes like 36HH, who say it's the first strapless bra that stayed up and fit true to size, often for a wedding. The one-star reviews say it ran large, gapped at the top or didn't lift a heavy bust, and one buyer wrote that expedited shipping took eight days. One Canadian reviewer said returns from Canada weren't accepted; the current policy accepts them for a CAD $14.95 fee.

Trustpilot has a single review, a two-star complaint that two bras in the same size fit very differently. One review isn't enough to rate the brand, but it matches the fit theme on the store.

Is Parfait legit?

Yes. It's an established brand that has sold since 2005, with a Shopify checkout, a shipping policy, a return policy, a returns portal and a support email. The fair question is fit, not whether your order will arrive: bra fit varies by style, and some reviewers needed to exchange sizes. Paying by card or PayPal gives you buyer protection.

Is it worth the money?

At $55 to $69, its bras cost about what other full-bust specialists charge, and less than many department-store brands in the same cup sizes. The value is in the size range: cups up to US L in a strapless longline are hard to find. The panty 6 packs ($49 and $52) bring the price per pair to about $8 to $9.
