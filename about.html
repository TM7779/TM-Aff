About

collects coupon codes and deals for independent online stores, so you can save a little without hunting through expired codes.

How we pick codes

We add codes from store newsletters, partner programs and our own purchases. Codes marked Verified were tested on the store's checkout. Expired codes are removed automatically.

How we write our guides

Each brand has a discount code guide with the current code, the brand's own prices and the cheapest way to buy: subscriptions, bundles, bigger sizes and free-shipping thresholds. We take prices and policies from the brand's own website and date every guide. We don't make health claims; for supplements, we suggest checking with your doctor first.
