Shipping and returns

Evilhair says it processes orders in 1 to 2 business days, then shipping takes 3 to 10 days, and up to 15 working days for most international parcels. All parcels can be tracked, though outside the EU only through your local post office. Shipping is free on orders over €115, its home page says. Holiday delivery can take 1 to 3 working days longer.

Returns are accepted within 25 days of delivery, for unused products in their original packaging bought on evilhair.com, with proof of purchase. Opened or used products can't be returned. Email its support within those 25 days to start a return; approved refunds go back to your original payment method within 8 days, Evilhair says.
