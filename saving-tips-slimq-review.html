The short answer

SlimQ is a real store with a US address in Burtonsville, Maryland. Its main chargers score 4.5 to 4.8 out of 5 on its own store, while its small Trustpilot page scores 2.6, with complaints about support and warranty help. The return rules are strict, so check compatibility before you buy.

What SlimQ sells

GaN gaming laptop chargers (150W, 240W and 330W with DC and USB-C ports), USB-C chargers from 30W to 150W, DC converters for different laptops, USB-C and MagSafe cables, and wireless chargers with night lights.

When we checked, prices ran from $34.99 for the 65W charger to $139.99 for the 330W.

Is SlimQ legit?

Yes. It lists a US address in Burtonsville, Maryland, and an email for support, ships worldwide with tracking, and says its chargers carry FCC, CE and UKCA certifications.

Is it worth the money?

If you carry a heavy gaming laptop brick, a SlimQ 240W or 330W charger is a lighter option at $109.99 to $139.99. Make sure your laptop's plug and wattage are supported before you order, because returns don't refund shipping and sale items can't be returned.
