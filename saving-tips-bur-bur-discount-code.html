Shipping and returns

BUR BUR says US orders take 3 to 4 business days to process and 3 to 5 business days to arrive, and orders after 2 p.m. Pacific are processed the next business day. When we checked, its product pages asked for 5 to 7 extra business days because of high order volume. It ships to 179 countries; international delivery takes 3 to 18 business days, and some countries, including Italy, Spain, Greece, Mexico and Vietnam, aren't served.

You have 14 days after delivery to request a return by emailing support. Items must be unopened and in their original packaging, and sale items and gift cards can't be returned. If the return is accepted, BUR BUR sends a return label; return shipping is refunded only when the item was faulty or the mistake was the brand's.
