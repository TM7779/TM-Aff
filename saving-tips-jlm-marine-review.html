The short answer

JLM Marine is a real manufacturer with a listed address in Shanghai, China, founded in 2002, that also ships from a US warehouse and Amazon FBA stock. Its best-selling kits score 4.7 to 5 out of 5 on its own store, but from only a handful of reviews each, and we found no Trustpilot page. Shipping is free; returns are strict, so match the part number first.

What JLM Marine sells

Aftermarket replacement parts for outboard and sterndrive engines from Yamaha, Mercury/Mariner, Honda, Suzuki, Johnson/Evinrude, Volvo Penta and MerCruiser: water pump impellers and repair kits, thermostats, carburetors and rebuild kits, fuel pumps, oil filters, sensors and switches, trim parts, cowl decal sets and boat accessories. It says its catalog has more than 7,000 parts.

JLM Marine says its parts are aftermarket replacements, not branded dealer OEM parts; brand names and OEM numbers are listed to show what each part fits. When we checked, its best sellers cost from $27.80 to $73.50.

Is JLM Marine legit?

Yes. It lists its company name, a phone number and an address in the Waigang industrial area of Shanghai, China, sells through a Shopify checkout with Shop Pay, PayPal and cards, publishes shipping and return policies, and offers fitment help by email before you order.

Is it worth the money?

If you service your own outboard, factory-direct aftermarket kits at $27.80 to $73.50 with free shipping can be a cheaper route than dealer parts. Weigh that against the few reviews and the strict returns: a change-of-mind return costs a 20% restocking fee plus shipping.

Boat owners and repair shops who do their own outboard maintenance, know their engine's model and part numbers, and want aftermarket kits for less than dealer prices. If you need genuine OEM parts, or aren't sure which part fits, ask JLM Marine or a marine mechanic before you order.
