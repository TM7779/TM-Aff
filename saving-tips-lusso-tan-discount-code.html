3. Check delivery times outside the UK

Returns

You can cancel an order within 14 days of receiving it by emailing Lusso Tan, then send it back within another 14 days. For hygiene reasons, tanning and skincare products can't be returned once their seal is broken. Faulty, damaged or wrong items are covered: email Lusso Tan with your order number and photos.
