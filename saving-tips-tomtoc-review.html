The short answer

tomtoc is a real bag and case brand, founded in 2016 by its own account, with free shipping and a 30-day return window. Store and Trustpilot ratings are strong, though some buyers report slow deliveries and trouble reaching support.

What tomtoc sells

Laptop sleeves and briefcases such as the Defender series, plus backpacks, sling bags, totes, pouches and packing cubes.

When we checked, the Defender-A14 briefcase was $40.99, the Navigator-T66 40L backpack was $89.99 and the X-Pac TechPack-T73 was $204.99.

Happy Trustpilot reviewers like the build of the bags and finding the right size. The complaints are about delivery and service: orders that went missing or sat in a warehouse, parts of an order that didn't arrive, slow replies, and one sleeve that was too small for a 16-inch laptop. tomtoc has claimed its Trustpilot page.

Is tomtoc legit?

Yes. tomtoc publishes its history on its About page, ships with named carriers (USPS, FedEx, UPS and Amazon Logistics), and has written return and warranty policies. The pages we checked don't name a head office.

Is it worth the money?

For a laptop you carry every day, the Defender cases start at about $41 and use tomtoc's CornerArmor corner protection. The X-Pac backpack costs far more, so it only makes sense if you want that material.
