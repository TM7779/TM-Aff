1. Judge the price, not the "was" price

Every best seller showed a price of €27 to €30 next to a "was" price of €110 to €115. That gap is the store's own figure, and we couldn't find the bags sold at €110 anywhere. Treat €27 as the real price and compare it with similar vegan leather bags.

2. Get the details right before you order

Returns cost you money here: you pay the return postage and Bond & Mason doesn't send a label. Check the size on each product page (the Palmer tote is 35 cm wide, the Bridget 19 cm) and the color before you pay, so you're less likely to send a bag back.

3. Order early for a gift

Orders take 1 to 2 working days to leave the warehouse and standard delivery 3 to 7 days, but the store says these are guidelines only and ships from warehouses around the world. Some Trustpilot reviewers say delivery took longer than they expected.

Shipping and returns

Standard shipping is free on most orders, and each order gets a tracking number by email or text.

You can return an unused bag in its original packaging within 30 days of delivery, through the returns center on the site. You pay return shipping, which isn't refunded, and bags sent to the wrong address aren't refunded. The home page also mentions a 5-year quality guarantee.
