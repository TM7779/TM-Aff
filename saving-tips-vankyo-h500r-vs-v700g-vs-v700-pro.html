The short version: the H500R is the cheapest way into native 1080p with streaming built in, the V700G Pro is the brightest and the best value per lumen, and the V700G and V700 Neo are near twins in the middle.

Choose the H500R for a dark room on a budget

At $199.99, or $179.99 with the code, the H500R is the lowest-priced of the four. It runs Roku OS, so the streaming apps are built in and the remote is a Roku TV remote. VANKYO lists 330 ANSI lumens, native 1920 × 1080, auto focus and auto keystone, two 5W speakers with Dolby Audio, dual-band Wi-Fi and two-way Bluetooth. It's also the quietest on paper, at 32 dB or less.

The limits are on the spec sheet too: one HDMI 1.4 port, 1 GB of RAM and a maximum picture of 150 inches. At 330 lumens it suits a bedroom, dorm or basement with the lights off. VANKYO also sells it with a carrying bag for $249.99.

V700G or V700 Neo for Google TV

If you'd rather have Google TV than Roku, the V700G ($298.00) and the V700 Neo ($299.99) are the next step up. Their spec sheets on ivankyo.com are almost the same: native 1080p, 400 ANSI lumens, HDR10+, two 8W speakers with Dolby Audio, 2 GB of RAM, 16 GB of storage, HDMI, USB and AV inputs, 38 dB of noise and the same 251 × 178 × 95 mm body.

The differences we found: the V700G's page lists automatic obstacle avoidance, automatic screen alignment and red-blue 3D support, while the V700 Neo's spec table lists "adjustable focus" (its feature list says auto focus and auto keystone). With $1.99 between them, the V700G lists more on paper for slightly less.

Choose the V700G Pro for brighter rooms

The V700G Pro is rated at 730 ANSI lumens, nearly twice the V700G, with a 1500:1 contrast ratio, and VANKYO pitches it for indoor and outdoor use. It has everything the V700G lists (Google TV, auto focus, keystone, obstacle avoidance and screen alignment, two 8W speakers) and comes with a 120-inch screen, an HDMI cable and a remote.

It costs $429.99, or $386.99 with the code. That looks like a big jump, but buying the V700G and VANKYO's StayTrue 120" screen separately comes to $427.99, so the Pro adds the extra brightness for about $2. It is also the cheapest of the four per lumen. Note that it's native 1080p with 4K input support, not a 4K projector.

Questions people ask

Which VANKYO projector is best?

It depends on your room. For a dark bedroom or basement on a budget, the H500R ($199.99, 330 ANSI lumens, Roku TV). For a brighter room or outdoor movies, the V700G Pro ($429.99, 730 ANSI lumens, 120" screen included). The V700G and V700 Neo sit in between at 400 lumens with Google TV.

VANKYO H500R vs V700G: what's the difference?

The H500R runs Roku TV, is rated at 330 ANSI lumens and costs $199.99. The V700G runs Google TV, is rated at 400 ANSI lumens, has two 8W speakers instead of two 5W, adds obstacle avoidance and screen alignment, and costs $298.00.

VANKYO V700G vs V700G Pro: what's the difference?

The V700G Pro is rated at 730 ANSI lumens against 400, has a 1500:1 contrast ratio against 1000:1, and comes with a 120-inch screen. It costs $429.99, against $298.00 for the V700G.

VANKYO V700 Neo vs V700G: which is better?

On VANKYO's spec sheets they are very close: native 1080p, 400 ANSI lumens, Google TV, two 8W speakers, 2 GB of RAM and 16 GB of storage. The V700G page also lists obstacle avoidance, screen alignment and red-blue 3D. The V700G was $298.00 and the V700 Neo $299.99.

How many lumens is the VANKYO H500R?

VANKYO lists the H500R at 330 ANSI lumens, with native 1920 × 1080 resolution and a 40" to 150" picture.

Is the VANKYO V700G Pro 4K?

No. VANKYO lists its native resolution as 1920 × 1080 and says it supports 4K input. The brand's 4K model on ivankyo.com is the Vista T4 laser projector.

Does the VANKYO H500R have Netflix?

The H500R runs Roku OS, so it streams through the Roku apps. The V700G, V700 Neo and V700G Pro run Google TV instead.

Which VANKYO projector comes with a screen?

The V700G Pro comes with a 120-inch screen. The Leisure 570B is also sold with a screen. For the others, VANKYO sells the StayTrue 120" portable screen for $129.99.
