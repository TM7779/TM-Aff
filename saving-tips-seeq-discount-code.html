What's in a serving

The product page lists 22 g of protein, 100 calories, 0 g sugar and 0 g lactose per serving, made with whey protein isolate and sweetened with monk fruit. Whey comes from milk, so check the label if you have a milk allergy, and talk to a doctor first if you're pregnant or take medication.

Shipping and returns

All SEEQ products are final sale: no returns or exchanges unless the item arrives damaged, defective or wrong. In that case, email support@seeqsupply.com.
