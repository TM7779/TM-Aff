All three have a 42.3 oz (2.5L) seed bin, white-light night view, two-way audio, a 9000mAh battery, an IP66 rating, a working range of 14°F to 113°F and a two-year warranty.

Choose the Feeder 2 for the lowest price

The Feeder 2 is the entry model: one wide-angle camera recording 1080p at 20 frames a second, a motion sensor that sends a "bird's here" alert to your phone, and Bird ID, which names the species, free for life in the Birdfy app. It had a 4.8 rating from 76 reviews on birdfy.com.

What you give up: there's no close-up portrait lens, no slow motion, and it connects only to 2.4GHz Wi-Fi. Birdfy's spec sheet lists the solar panel as optional for this model, so check the product page's What's Included if you don't want to bring the camera in to charge.

Choose the Feeder 2 Pro for close-ups

The Pro keeps the same feeder but swaps in a dual-lens camera. The upper portrait lens tracks the bird and frames it automatically, at 1536×1536, and can switch to 100 frames a second for slow-motion replays. The lower wide lens records up to 2304×1296 in high-quality mode, which Birdfy sells as 2K.

For $30 more than the Feeder 2 when we checked, it also comes with a 3W solar panel, and newer units add 5GHz Wi-Fi and Bluetooth pairing for setup. Birdfy also sells it in a bundle with a year of its Plus membership for $259.99.

Choose the Feeder 2 Duo for a second angle

The Duo is a Feeder 2 Pro with a second camera mounted on the side, so you see each visit from the front, in close-up and from the side, where Birdfy says you catch birds' interactions. Each camera has its own 9000mAh battery, and the Duo comes with a bigger 5W solar panel.

The side camera on older units records 1080p at 20fps; newer units record up to 2304×1296. At $329.99, it cost $130 more than the Pro when we checked, so it's for people who want the extra angle, not just better pictures.

Do you need a membership?

No. Birdfy says every feeder comes with Bird ID free for life, along with live view, alerts, auto-recorded clips and a monthly recap in its app. Its paid Plus membership (from $0.19 a day, billed yearly) adds unlimited photo ID, details like sexes and juveniles, 15% off future Birdfy purchases and up to 2 devices; Pro membership (from $0.41 a day) goes further. None of the Feeder 2 models has a micro SD card slot: clips are stored in the cloud.

Questions people ask

What is the difference between Birdfy Feeder 2 and Feeder 2 Pro?

The Feeder 2 has one 1080p wide-angle lens. The Pro adds a second, portrait lens that tracks birds automatically, with 2K video from its wide lens in high-quality mode, a 3W solar panel in the box and, on the newer version, 5GHz Wi-Fi and Bluetooth pairing.

What is the difference between Birdfy Feeder 2 Pro and Duo?

The Duo is a Feeder 2 Pro with a second camera on the side, so it records from three lenses and three angles. It comes with a 5W solar panel instead of 3W, weighs 3.9 lbs instead of 3.2 lbs, and cost $130 more when we checked.

Is the Birdfy Feeder 2 Pro worth it?

For most buyers it's the middle choice: $30 more than the Feeder 2 for a second lens with auto-tracking, sharper video and a solar panel in the box, and $130 less than the Duo.

Which Birdfy feeder is best?

It depends on what you want to watch. The Feeder 2 ($169.99) covers alerts, live view and free Bird ID; the Pro ($199.99) adds close-up portrait video; the Duo ($329.99) adds a side view of the feeder.

Does the Birdfy Feeder 2 need a subscription?

No. Birdfy says Bird ID, live view, alerts and auto-recorded clips are free in its app for life. Paid Plus and Pro memberships add details like sexes and juveniles, unlimited photo ID and longer clips.

Does the Birdfy Feeder 2 have an SD card slot?

No. Birdfy's spec sheet says micro SD card replay isn't available on any Feeder 2 model; clips are stored in the cloud.

Does the Birdfy Feeder 2 work with 5GHz Wi-Fi?

The Feeder 2 uses 2.4GHz Wi-Fi only. Newer versions of the Pro and Duo support 2.4GHz and 5GHz; older ones are 2.4GHz only.

Does the Birdfy Feeder 2 come with a solar panel?

Birdfy's spec sheet lists the solar panel as optional for the Feeder 2, and includes a 3W panel with the Pro and a 5W panel with the Duo. Check the product page's What's Included before you buy.
