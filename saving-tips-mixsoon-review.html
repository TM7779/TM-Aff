The short answer

mixsoon is a real Korean skincare brand with a US store that ships from California. Its Bean Essence is rated 4.5 out of 5 from 405 reviews on mixsoon.us and its Bean Cream 4.8 from 205. The Bean Sunscreen scores lower, at 3.5 from 460. Returns are short: 14 days, unopened only.

What mixsoon sells

Essences, serums, creams, sunscreens, cleansers, sheet and hydrogel masks, plus an "and by mixsoon" hair and body line. The best-known range is built on fermented soybean (the Bean line); others use centella, purple rice, bifida ferment and PDRN.

When we checked, the best sellers cost $35.00 for the 50ml Bean Cream or Bean Essence and $28.00 for the 50ml Bean Sunscreen. The brand says most of its products meet vegan standards.

Is mixsoon legit?

Yes. mixsoon.us lists a US address (Compton, California), ships from US storage by UPS and USPS, publishes its shipping and refund policies and answers at a customer service email. The brand also lists official retailers by country, including YesStyle and Stylevana, and sells on Amazon. If you buy elsewhere, check that list first, since a return to mixsoon only covers its own store.

Is it worth the money?

At $35.00 for 50ml, the Bean Essence and Bean Cream sit in the middle of Korean skincare prices, and the store ratings for both are high. The duo set and free shipping over $60 lower the cost. The 14-day, unopened-only return means you can't send something back after trying it, so start with one product if you're unsure.
