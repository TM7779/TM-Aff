Choose the MSG100 for one door

The MSG100 is a small box (3.7 × 1.8 × 0.9 inches) that wires to your garage door opener's wall button terminals, with one sensor on a 23-foot wire to tell it whether the door is open or shut. From the Meross app or Apple Home you can open and close the door from anywhere, and the app sends alerts when the door opens or closes, when it stays open too long, and at night if it's still open. It also keeps a log of every open and close.

Meross lists 2.4GHz Wi-Fi only, with no support for enterprise Wi-Fi, and an operating temperature of 32 to 104°F. The antenna is inside the case, so if your router is far from the garage, check the Wi-Fi signal by the opener with your phone first. It has the reviews to go with it: 4.78 out of 5 from 172 reviews on Meross's store.

Choose the MSG200HK for two or three doors

The MSG200HK does the same job for up to three doors from one box. Meross calls it the "new model" and gives it an external antenna for a stronger Wi-Fi signal, plus a built-in sound alarm that Meross says makes closing the door safer for homes with children and pets. Two sets of sensor cables come in the box; for a third door you add the $16.99 Additional Kit, a single sensor on a 23-foot cable that only works with the MSG200 or MSG200HK.

It works with the same apps and voice assistants as the MSG100, with no subscription. Its product page showed no star rating when we checked, so there's less store feedback to go on than for the MSG100.

Check compatibility first

For the US and Canada, Meross asks buyers to run the compatibility check on its website before buying. Its quick test: short the wall button terminals with pliers, and if the door moves, the opener works directly. If not, you may need an accessory; Meross says to send it your opener's learn button color and it will ship the right accessory free. For other countries it says the devices fit more than 99% of garage door openers with no accessory.

Meross also sells the RGR910 remote accessory ($14.99) for Chamberlain, LiftMaster, Genie and other openers on 300 to 390MHz. It lists the RGR910 as not working with white "Security 3.0" learn buttons, or with Allstar, Marantec and Hormann openers. A third model, the MSG150 ($69.99), is described in Meross's store data as built for Chamberlain, LiftMaster and Craftsman, but its product page didn't open when we checked.

Questions people ask

What's the difference between the Meross MSG100 and MSG200?

The MSG100 controls one garage door, has an internal antenna and costs $49.99. The MSG200HK controls up to three doors, has an external antenna, comes with two sensor sets and costs $69.99 on shop.meross.com.

Meross MSG100 vs MSG200HK: which should I buy?

Buy the MSG100 for a single garage door. Buy the MSG200HK if you have two or three doors, or if your Wi-Fi is weak in the garage, since it has an external antenna.

How many garage doors can the Meross MSG200 control?

Up to three at the same time, according to Meross. Two sensor sets come in the box; for a third door you add the Additional Kit sensor, $16.99.

Does the Meross garage door opener work with Apple HomeKit?

Meross's store lists both the MSG100 and the MSG200HK as working with the Apple Home app and Siri, as well as Amazon Alexa and Google Assistant.

Is the Meross garage door opener compatible with my opener?

In the US and Canada, Meross asks you to run its compatibility check first. If you need an accessory, you send Meross your opener's learn button color and it ships the accessory free. Elsewhere it says the devices fit more than 99% of openers.

What is the Meross MSG150?

The MSG150 is listed at $69.99 in Meross's store data and described as built for Chamberlain, LiftMaster and Craftsman openers. Its product page didn't open when we checked on November 15, 2026.

Does Meross charge a subscription for the garage door opener?

No. Meross says there's no monthly fee or other subscription fee for the MSG100 or the MSG200HK.

Does the Meross MSG100 work on 5GHz Wi-Fi?

No. Meross lists 2.4GHz Wi-Fi (802.11 b/g/n) only, and it doesn't support enterprise Wi-Fi.

What warranty does Meross give?

Meross's limited warranty for a new product lasts 1 year. Its policy says smart devices count down from activation.
