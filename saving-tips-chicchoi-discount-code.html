Shipping and returns

All orders ship from China to more than 60 countries. ChicChoi says it packs orders in 1 to 4 business days, then delivery takes 8 to 14 business days to the US, 6 to 8 to Australia, Canada, New Zealand and Singapore, and 6 to 10 to the UK and most of the EU. Every parcel is tracked. Shipping is priced by weight and destination at checkout, with free-shipping promotions now and then. For US, UK and EU orders, ChicChoi says it pays import duties and VAT, so there's nothing extra to pay on delivery.

For a change of mind, contact ChicChoi within 30 days of delivery. You pay the return postage to China, shipping fees aren't refunded and a 10% restocking fee is taken off. Damaged or faulty items are handled within 30 days of delivery by replacement or another fix. Refunds can go back to your card or PayPal, or as store credit with a 5% bonus.
