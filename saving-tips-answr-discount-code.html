ANSWR review: the short answer

ANSWR is a real company with a working Shopify store, published shipping and refund policies and a 30-day money-back guarantee. Trustpilot showed a 3.9 TrustScore from about 1,750 reviews when we checked, and its own review page lists 2,247 reviews. Most buyers like how smooth their hair turned out; the complaints that repeat are slow dispatch, results that didn't last for some hair types, and slow replies to refund requests.

What ANSWR sells

Home versions of salon treatments. The main one is the at-home keratin treatment (£37.90 a bottle): you spray it on clean, dry hair, leave it for an hour, rinse, dry and seal it with a straightener at 180°C. ANSWR says it is formaldehyde-free, vegan, and lasts up to three months from one application; those are the brand's claims. It also sells a kit with post-treatment shampoo and conditioner (£49.90), the shampoo and conditioner in 300ml bottles (£39.90), an exfoliating hair removal drop (£24.90), a flyaway and brow control stick (£17.90), the Volumewave heated brush (£79.90) and DIY lash extension kits.

The keratin treatment's ingredient list includes glyoxylic acid and fragrance. If you have sensitive skin, read the full list on the product page first, and the brand suggests covering your shoulders with an old towel because the formula can mark coloured fabric.
