The short answer

Evilhair is a real brand with a working shop, published policies and hundreds of reviews on its own product pages, which rate its colors 4.3 to 4.8 out of 5. But its Trustpilot score is 1.7 out of 5 from 39 reviews, and most of those are about orders that arrived weeks late or not at all, and emails that went unanswered. The product gets good marks; the delivery and support are the risk.

What Evilhair sells

The main product is ColorConditioner: you apply it like a conditioner after shampooing, leave it on, and rinse it out. It comes in Pastel shades (silver, lavender, rose, peach) and Intense shades (violet, magenta, cherry red, royal blue, ash silver), in 100 ml for €15.99 or 200 ml for €23.99, plus color shampoos and bundles. Evilhair says the colors are vegan and not tested on animals, that Intense colors last up to 12 washes and Pastel up to 7, and that they show best on light blonde or bleached hair.

The two sources tell different stories. Reviews on Evilhair's own product pages come from people who received the product and mostly like the color. On Trustpilot, the recent reviews, from the US, the UK and across Europe, are nearly all 1 star: orders that never arrived or took a month or more, emails and chat messages with no reply, and refunds that were hard to get. A few reviewers say the product itself is good and smells nice, but that support is poor. Read the latest Trustpilot reviews before you order.

Is Evilhair legit?

It's a real business, not a fake shop: it sells its own products, publishes shipping, return and application instructions, gives customer service hours (Monday to Friday, 10:00 to 18:00 Central European Time), and has claimed its Trustpilot profile, which lists an address in Limassol, Cyprus. Prices are in euros and the site comes in English, German and Italian. The many reports of orders that never arrived are a real concern, so pay with a card or PayPal that offers buyer protection, keep your order confirmation and tracking number, and contact support early if a parcel doesn't move.

Is it worth the money?

At €23.99 for 200 ml, ColorConditioner costs about the same as other semi-permanent color conditioners, and the 200 ml bottle and bundles bring the price per use down. The color itself rates well with buyers who received it. What you pay for is the risk on delivery: if you need the color by a certain date, or you can buy the same brand from a seller near you, that may be the safer choice.
