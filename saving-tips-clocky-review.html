The short answer

Clocky is a real product from a real company, Clocky, LLC, and it sells direct at clocky.com and through its own Amazon stores. Its product page shows 4.7 out of 5 stars from 3,593 reviews. Most complaints are about clocks that stop rolling or stop beeping after some weeks, and the warranty is only 90 days, so keep your receipt.

What Clocky sells

The store is built around one product: the Clocky alarm clock, $31.99 in black or blue and $36.99 in white or lavender on November 15, 2026. When the alarm goes off you can snooze once (for 1 to 9 minutes) or not at all; then it jumps from the nightstand, up to 3 feet high, and rolls around the room on carpet or wood, beeping with a robot-like sound. You can also switch the wheels off and use it as a regular alarm clock, and it shows 12- or 24-hour time.

Beyond the clock, the store sells a two-clock bundle ($55), a 3-year extended warranty ($19.99), a "Nap Sleep Snooze" throw pillow ($23.99 and $26.99) and graphic tees ($19.99). Several colors, an eye mask and a phone dock were sold out that day.

The score is high, but most of the reviews shown on the product page are from 2023 or earlier, so they say more about the clock's long track record than about recent batches. Happy buyers mostly write that it finally gets them, or their teenagers, out of bed. One long-time owner liked it but complained that it sometimes gets stuck under the bed.

The low-star reviews describe clocks that stopped rolling after a few days, stopped making sound after about a week, or reset. Another buyer said they couldn't find the Duracell lithium AAA batteries the manual asks for. Clocky's FAQ puts many of these problems down to batteries and suggests new lithium ones and a tight battery door, but if yours still fails, use the warranty.

Is Clocky legit?

Yes. Clocky, LLC runs clocky.com and lists a phone number, (734) 999-0584, and the email team@clocky.com on its contact page. The store publishes its shipping, return and warranty rules, a downloadable instruction manual and a store locator, and it sells under the CLOCKY name on Amazon in more than ten countries.

The brand warns that look-alike rolling alarm clocks are sold elsewhere and says the original is the one sold by the seller "CLOCKY". Buying from clocky.com or Clocky's own Amazon store is the way to be sure you get the original and the warranty.

Is it worth the money?

At $31.99, or $23.99 with our code, Clocky costs more than a basic alarm clock but less than most smart alarm clocks. It's worth it if you keep turning off your phone alarm in your sleep, since you have to get out of bed to stop it. It's less of a fit if you share a room, have a dog that reacts to it, or have a cluttered floor where it can get stuck.
