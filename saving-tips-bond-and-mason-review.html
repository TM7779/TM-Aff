The short answer

Bond & Mason is a real store that ships orders: 2,543 Trustpilot reviews average 4.2 out of 5, and most buyers say the bags look good for the price. But about 1 in 8 reviews gives 1 star, mostly over quality and returns, and the "was €110" prices next to €27 bags shouldn't be read as the bags' value. Buy it as a low-cost bag, not a luxury one.

What Bond & Mason sells

The best sellers when we checked were handbags and totes priced €27 to €30: the Palmer Tote Bag, the Christy, Bridget, Alicia and Amari handbags, the Fiona Clutch Bag and the Havana Shoulder Bag. The product pages list 100% vegan leather, a fabric lining and gold- or silver-tone hardware, with sizes in centimeters and inches.

Most reviews are short and positive: pretty bags, good material for the price, an easy website, and some repeat buyers on their fifth or sixth order. Some say delivery took longer than expected but the bag was worth the wait.

The negative reviews say the bags look and feel cheap for what was paid, with one comparing them to Temu, and that the returns process is hard. That matches the policy: you pay return postage and no label is provided.

Is Bond & Mason legit?

It's a working store with a claimed Trustpilot profile and thousands of buyers, so orders do arrive. What it doesn't show is a business address: the contact page is empty, Trustpilot lists the Netherlands, and the store's account lists Sheung Wan, Hong Kong. It ships from warehouses around the world.

The "was" prices of €110 to €115 next to €27 sale prices are the store's own figures. We couldn't find these bags sold at those prices, so judge them at €27.

Is it worth the money?

At €27 to €30, with free shipping on most orders, a Bond & Mason bag costs about what a high-street vegan leather bag costs on sale. The risk is the return: if you don't like it, postage back is yours, which can eat a good part of a €27 refund.
