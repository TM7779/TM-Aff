The short answer

Webber Naturals is a long-standing Canadian company, founded in 1948, that says its products are formulated, made and tested in British Columbia. Prices are moderate, the online store ships within Canada, and independent store reviews are few.

What Webber Naturals sells

Single vitamins and minerals (A, B, C, D, E, K, iron, magnesium, zinc), multivitamins, omega-3s, collagen, probiotics, melatonin products and herbs such as turmeric and ashwagandha, plus the PGX fibre range.

When we checked, most single vitamins were C$16 to C$19 and the Triple Strength Omega-3 C$29.99 to C$38.99.
