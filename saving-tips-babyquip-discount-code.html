On top of the rental, you pay the provider's delivery fee and BabyQuip's service fee of 6 to 9%, worked out on the rental and delivery subtotal. Your order summary shows both before you pay.

1. Rent for 9 days or more

BabyQuip takes money off longer rentals automatically, with no code needed:

  • 1 to 8 days: the standard daily rate
  • 9 to 16 days: 10% off
  • 17 to 24 days: 20% off
  • 25 days or more: 30% off

BabyQuip counts every calendar day you have the gear, so Monday evening to the next Monday morning is 8 days. If your trip is 8 days, check whether one extra day moves you into the 10% tier: a crib from $14 a day costs $112 for 8 days and $113.40 for 9 days with 10% off.

2. Pick a provider with self-pickup

Delivery, setup and pickup are included in the delivery fee, which each provider sets by distance and area. Some providers offer self-pickup and self-return instead: you collect the gear from their address and bring it back. If you have a car and time, it can save the delivery fee. Look for providers that offer it before you book, and note that a full-size folding crib doesn't fit in every car.

Airport deliveries, same-day deliveries, after-hours drop-offs and holiday deliveries can cost extra with some providers.

3. Book everything in one order

If a provider has to come back, for example because you add gear after the first delivery, there's an extra trip fee. Add the crib, stroller, high chair and anything else to the same reservation, with the same provider, so you pay one delivery fee.

4. Know the cancellation rules before you pay

BabyQuip takes full payment when you book. What you get back if plans change depends on when you cancel:

  • Within 24 hours of booking, and 48 hours or more before the start: a full refund, minus the service fee.
  • 15 days or more before the start: the rental fees are refunded, minus the service fee.
  • Less than 15 days before the start: rental fees aren't refunded, but delivery fees, surcharges and prepaid tips are.
  • Orders made less than 24 hours before the start: no refund.

Cancel For Any Reason is an optional add-on for reservations with US providers. With it, you can cancel up to the start time and get your rental and service fees back, minus the cost of the add-on. It can't be added within 48 hours of the start.

Other fees to know

  • Minimum rental: usually 3 days. Some providers allow 1 or 2 days if you ask.
  • Damage: in the US, if gear is damaged, lost or stolen and the provider files a claim, you pay a $25 minor damage charge and BabyQuip covers accidental damage up to $1,000.
  • Cleaning: gear returned with vomit, smoke or heavy stains can cost $25 an item, or full replacement.
  • Car seats: providers can't install them, for insurance reasons. You get the manual or a link to the instructions.
