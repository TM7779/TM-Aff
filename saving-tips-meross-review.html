The short answer

Meross is a real smart home brand with a 45-day return window and free shipping, but its Trustpilot page scores only 2.6 out of 5, mainly over setup and customer service. Its devices are cheap ways to add Apple Home, Alexa or Google control.

What Meross sells

Garage door openers, plugs, presence sensors, smoke alarms, hubs and other Wi-Fi and Matter devices. Meross says they work with Matter, Apple HomeKit, Alexa, Google Assistant and SmartThings.

When we checked, the MSG100 garage door opener was $49.99 in the US version and the MS600 presence sensor was $40.99.

Yes. Meross says it was founded in 2016, publishes a warranty and refund policy, and ships through Amazon's logistics in the US, Canada and Australia. The low Trustpilot score is about experience with products and support, not about a fake store.

The prices are low for the features if your router and home system are supported. Because the complaints center on setup, read the compatibility notes first and use the 45-day window to test the device properly.
