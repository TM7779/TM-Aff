Shipping times

Elliosa is based in the UK. Orders are processed in 1 to 3 business days, or 1 day with Priority Processing. Delivery then takes 3 to 6 business days in the UK, 4 to 8 to the US, Europe, Australia and Canada, and 5 to 9 elsewhere.

Returns

You have 30 days from delivery to return unused socks in their original packaging. Outside the UK you pay the return postage yourself, so check the size guide before you order.
