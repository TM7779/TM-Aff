Sale price or code: how to pay less

Most stores accept one discount per order, and a code often won't take more off a price that's already reduced. So compare the two:

  • Where a brand has a sale, add the item to your cart, try our code, and keep whichever total is lower. Carpuride's Black Friday sale is 32% off against 30% with our code; WAVwatch's early price is 20% off the WAVwatch 2.2 against 15% with our code; AVEA's offer is an extra 30% off against 15%.
  • Where a brand shows no sale, the code is the saving to use, and prices are the same as any other week.
  • Watch for gifts and thresholds. Carpuride adds 2 free gifts on orders of $49.90 or more, and AVEA adds a free Welcome Kit and a full-size product. A gift can be worth more than a few extra percent off.
  • Check the return rules before Black Friday. Some brands treat sale items as final sale. Each brand's discount code guide on this site lists its return policy.

How we check these deals

Once a day until Cyber Monday, we read each brand's own home page and note any Black Friday, Cyber Monday or holiday offer it shows, in plain words. We don't copy offers from other coupon sites, we don't place test orders, and we never call a code verified. Questions people ask

When is Black Friday 2026?

Friday, November 27, 2026, the day after Thanksgiving in the US. Many brands on this page started earlier: TABASQUISHY's Black Friday and Cyber Monday deals run from November 19, and EPOMAKER's sale from November 16.

When is Cyber Monday 2026?

Monday, November 30, 2026. Some sales run past it: EPOMAKER's goes to December 3, 2026 (GMT+8).

Can I use a discount code on Black Friday sale items?

Usually only one discount applies to an order, and many stores don't let a code take more off a sale price. Put the item in your cart, try the code, and keep whichever total is lower.

Is a Black Friday sale cheaper than a discount code?

It depends on the brand. Carpuride's Black Friday sale is 32% off against 30% with our code, and WAVwatch's early price is 20% off against 15%. Where a brand shows no sale, the code is the bigger saving.

Which brands have a Black Friday sale in 2026?

Which brands give free gifts for Black Friday?

AVEA adds a free Welcome Kit and a free full-size product to its Black Friday offer, and Carpuride adds 2 free gifts on orders of $49.90 or more, as shown on their sites on November 18, 2026.

Are these codes verified?
