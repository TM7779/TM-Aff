The short answer

Tidradio is a real radio brand with low prices and a free shipping offer, but it has very few public reviews. Its Trustpilot score of 2.6 comes from just 4 reviews, and its 12-day return window is short.

What Tidradio sells

Handheld ham and GMRS radios such as the TD-H3 Plus, TD-H8 and TD-H9, plus 4G push-to-talk radios such as the TD-M15 that use a SIM card, and accessories like batteries and earpieces.

When we checked, a single TD-H3 Plus was $29.99, a TD-H8 $59.99 and a TD-H9 $69.99. The TD-638 was sold out.

Is Tidradio legit?

It looks like the brand's own store, with its own model line, written shipping and return policies and support. The pages we checked don't name a company address, and warranty labels vary (we saw 1 year and 360 days), so confirm yours before you buy.

Is it worth the money?

The prices are low for what's listed, especially the TD-H3 Plus. With few reviews and only 12 days for returns, test the radio as soon as it arrives and check you can use the bands legally where you live.
