The short answer

PlayVital is a real gaming accessory company. Its About page names PlayVital Limited with an address in Hung Hom, Kowloon, Hong Kong, and its store listed 1,234 products when we checked. Prices are low, from $9.99 for thumb grip caps to $32.99 for a metal wall mount, and shipping is free worldwide. The trade-off is time: orders ship from China, and PlayVital itself puts US delivery at 15 to 25 days.

What PlayVital sells

Most of the store is accessories for Nintendo Switch 2: hard and dockable cases in many themed series, silicone covers for the Switch 2 Pro Controller, thumb grip caps, skin decals, tempered glass screen protectors, carrying cases and dust covers. It also sells grips and covers for the original Switch, Steam Deck pads, Xbox Series X|S and Xbox Elite Series 2 controller covers, and a universal controller wall mount.

When we checked, its best sellers were the Switch 2 Pro Controller grip skin ($14.99), the metal wall mount with 7 game card slots ($32.99), the Cute Solid-Color Switch 2 case ($23.99), textured rubber grips for Switch ($14.99) and Steam Deck grip pads ($13.99).

Is it worth the money?
