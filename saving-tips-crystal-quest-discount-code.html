1. Reach $150 for free shipping

Crystal Quest's site shows free shipping on orders over $150, with exclusions it doesn't list on the banner. Below that, shipping is worked out at checkout from the weight, the address and the method you pick.

A single under-sink system at $149.90 sits just under the line. Adding a spare cartridge, like an $18 pitcher cartridge or a $29.50 shower cartridge, puts most carts over $150. The site doesn't say whether the $150 is counted before or after the code: $150 after 15% off is about $176.47 before it, so check the shipping line after you enter the code.

2. Subscribe to replacement cartridges

Filters need new cartridges, and that is where most of the long-term cost sits. Crystal Quest's replacement cartridges have a Subscribe & Save option: 10% off when they're delivered every 3 months, and 5% off for every 6 months, every 9 months or once a year.

Crystal Quest lists the pitcher cartridge at 2,000 gallons or 6 to 12 months, and the shower filter cartridge at 6 to 12 months, so most homes would pick the 6-month or yearly delivery. Packs cost less per cartridge too: the Standard/SMART pitcher cartridge is $18 alone or $101.95 for six (about $16.99 each), and the shower cartridge is $29.50 alone or $55.99 for two.

3. Start with a single cartridge system

The countertop and under-sink SMART systems come with one, two or three cartridges. The single versions cost the least: $86.90 for the white countertop filter and $149.90 for the under-sink filter. Crystal Quest says each provides about 10,000 gallons, or 1 to 3 years, before the flow slows or stops to show the cartridge needs changing.

Crystal Quest sells city water and well water tests from $159. If you aren't sure which cartridge you need, the store's refund policy asks you to know your water before you buy, because returns cost money (see below).

Shipping and returns

Orders are processed within 1 business day, and shipping methods and delivery estimates are shown at checkout. Crystal Quest is based in Kennesaw, Georgia, and its shipping policy also covers orders to Canada and the UK, where taxes and import fees can apply.

Returns are strict, so read them first. You have 30 days from delivery. A refund is only given on new, unused items in their original packaging with no missing parts, you pay the return shipping, the original shipping isn't refunded, and returns can carry a restocking fee of up to 30%. Custom and special orders can't be returned. Exchanges are accepted for faulty items and upgrades.
