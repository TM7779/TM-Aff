Choose the EM11 NL for smaller hands and a low price

The EM11 NL is the slim one. ProtoArc says it's shaped for small to medium hands under 7.5 inches, with an extended rest under the palm and a striped, non-slip grip. It has quiet left and right clicks, forward and back buttons on the side, and a DPI button with three steps: 1000, 1600 and 2400. It measures 68.1 mm high, 69.2 mm wide and 138 mm long, and weighs 112 g.

What it doesn't do: nothing on it can be remapped, and ProtoArc says the side buttons don't work on macOS at all. If you're on a Mac and use back and forward a lot, that matters. At $20.99 in any of 11 colors, it's an inexpensive way to find out whether a vertical mouse suits you.

Choose the EM11 Pro for larger hands and more control

The EM11 Pro is the full-size version, for hands of 7.5 to 8.5 inches. ProtoArc says its larger shape gives the palm fuller support, at a 58° vertical angle. It adds a horizontal side scroll wheel, which helps in wide spreadsheets, video timelines and long documents, and DPI up to 7200 for big or several screens.

You set it up in the ProtoArc Web HUB, a web page that runs in Chrome or Edge, with no software to install: 6 buttons, the DPI and the side wheel can be changed, and the settings save to the mouse itself. On a Mac, macOS asks you to allow Input Monitoring for the browser first. The Pro lists Windows 8 or later, macOS 10.12 or later and Android 4.3 or later. ProtoArc says first-time vertical mouse users take about one to two weeks to get used to it.

What both mice share

Both connect to three devices at once, two over Bluetooth 5.0 and one through the USB-A receiver in the box, and switch with a button. Both charge a 500mAh battery over USB-C, and both have quiet clicks. If you only have USB-C ports, you'll need an adapter for the receiver, or use Bluetooth.

Questions people ask

What is the difference between the ProtoArc EM11 NL and EM11 Pro?

The EM11 Pro is the bigger mouse, for hands of 7.5 to 8.5 inches, with up to 7200 DPI, a side scroll wheel and 6 buttons you can remap in a web browser. The EM11 NL is slimmer, for hands under 7.5 inches, with 1000, 1600 or 2400 DPI and no customization. They cost $35.99 and $20.99 on protoarc.com.

Is the ProtoArc EM11 Pro worth it?

If your hand is 7.5 inches or longer, or you scroll sideways through spreadsheets and timelines a lot, the $15 extra buys the size and the side wheel. For a smaller hand or simple office work, the EM11 NL does the same basic job for less.

What hand size is the ProtoArc EM11 NL for?

ProtoArc says the EM11 NL is optimized for small to medium hands under 7.5 inches (about 19 cm), measured from the base of the palm to the tip of the middle finger.

What hand size is the ProtoArc EM11 Pro for?

Hand lengths of 7.5 to 8.5 inches (19.0 to 21.5 cm), ProtoArc says. It calls the EM11 Pro a full-size vertical mouse made for large hands.

Does the ProtoArc EM11 NL work with a Mac?

Yes, it lists macOS 10.12 or later, but ProtoArc says its forward and back side buttons don't work on macOS and can't be programmed.

Do I need software for the EM11 Pro?

No. You change its buttons, side scroll wheel and DPI in the ProtoArc Web HUB in Chrome or Edge, and the settings save to the mouse. On a Mac, you first allow Input Monitoring for the browser in System Settings.

How many devices can the ProtoArc EM11 connect to?

Both connect to 3 devices: two over Bluetooth 5.0 and one through the included 2.4 GHz USB-A receiver, with a button to switch between them.

How do you charge the ProtoArc EM11?

Both have a built-in 500mAh battery that charges with the included USB-C cable. On the EM11 Pro the cable charges only and doesn't carry data.
