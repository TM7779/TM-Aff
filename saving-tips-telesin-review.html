The short answer

Telesin is a real company: Shenzhen Telesin Digital Co., Ltd., founded in 2013, with its own factory in Dongguan, China. Its store publishes shipping, return and warranty policies, and its most-reviewed products are rated between 4.8 and 5 out of 5 on its own product pages. Most items cost $14 to $64, a fraction of the camera brands' own accessories. The two things to know before you buy: shipping usually takes 2 to 4 weeks, and the return policy excludes sale items.

The reviews on Telesin's store are mostly short and positive. Buyers of the GoPro case say it kept water out at sea and costs far less than GoPro's own housing; buyers of the 3m selfie stick praise it for filming underwater; and several reviewers mention quick email replies from support.

We found no reviews under four stars on these product pages, which is worth keeping in mind: the reviews are shown by the store itself. The milder comments we read were about delivery (one buyer said the case took a long time to arrive) and weight (one said the diving phone case is a little heavy to use out of the water).

Is Telesin legit?

Yes. The store's About page says Telesin is owned by Shenzhen Telesin Digital Co., Ltd., founded in 2013, with headquarters in Shenzhen, a registered address in Wan Chai, Hong Kong, and its own factory in the Dalang Innovation Industrial Park in Dongguan. It lists 248 intellectual property items, including 6 invention patents, and says it works with DJI, Insta360 and other camera brands.

The store publishes a shipping policy, a 30-day return policy and a one-year warranty, and gives support email addresses with hours (Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM to 6:30 PM, China time). People also search "telesin store" with a country name: Telesin is sold by many shops and marketplaces, but its warranty page says it can only give after-sales support for orders on its official website.

Is it worth the money?

For GoPro and DJI owners, Telesin gear costs much less than the camera brands' own accessories: a waterproof case for GoPro Hero 9 to 13 is $13.99, a floating stick $24.99 and a chest belt $13.99. The phone gear is pricier, with the Master Grip Kit for iPhone 18 Pro at $71.99 and kits with lenses up to $229.99.

The trade-offs are time and returns. Standard shipping usually takes 2 to 4 weeks, so order well before a trip, and the return policy excludes sale items, which on November 18, 2026 covered most of the store. If you're unsure an item fits your camera or phone, check the model list on its page first.

Who it's for

Telesin suits GoPro, DJI and Insta360 owners who want cases, floats and mounts without paying camera-brand prices, snorkelers and divers on a budget, and phone filmmakers who want a grip or a neck mount. It's a poor fit if you need gear next week, or if you want to be able to return an item bought on sale.
