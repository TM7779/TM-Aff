1. Most orders ship free, with no minimum

Kore Kawaii ships fashion, accessories and decor free. Only anime figures, snacks and drinks are excluded, and those ship to the US only. So a single $15.99 bunny keychain ships free, and you don't need to add extra items to reach a threshold.

Orders take 1 to 3 days to process, then 5 to 12 days to arrive in the US, UK, Canada, Australia and most of Europe. For a birthday or holiday gift, order at least two weeks ahead.

2. Teachers get 20% off

Kore Kawaii runs a Teacher Discount Program: verified educators get 20% off, which beats the 10% code. Most stores accept one code per order, so if you're a teacher, use whichever saves more.

3. Check the return rules before buying blind boxes

You can return items within 14 days of delivery if they're new, unused and still tagged. Ask customer service first: returns sent without approval aren't processed, and once approved, Kore Kawaii sends you a return label. Blind boxes can't be returned, so only buy one if you'd be happy with any figure in the series.
