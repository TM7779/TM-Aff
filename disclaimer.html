How affiliate links work

When you select Shop, Show code or a product on , the store's website opens through a tracking link from the store or its affiliate partner. If you buy, the store pays us a small share of the sale. Some codes are also linked to us, so the store knows the sale came from our site even without the link.

The store or its partner may use cookies to record that you came from us. If you block cookies or browse privately, the code still works for you; we just may not be credited.

Which links earn us money

Shop buttons, the codes we list, products in Shop the favourites, and store links inside our guides and reviews. Links to other sites we mention, such as Trustpilot, don't earn us anything.

What commissions don't change

  • The price you pay. Commissions come out of the store's share, not your cart.
  • What we write. We report public ratings as we find them, low ones included, and list the cons as well as the pros.
  • Which code we show. We show the best working code we have for each store.

To be upfront: we only list stores we have a partnership with, so isn't a list of every brand out there.

How we write guides and reviews

Our guides and reviews bring together each brand's own product details, prices and policies, plus public ratings, with the date we checked them. We haven't lab-tested the products unless a page says so. Prices are in the store's currency at the time of writing.

Codes, prices and availability

  • Stores set their own prices, discounts and expiry dates, and can change them at any time.
  • Some codes exclude sale items, bundles or subscriptions, or work only once per customer.
  • Codes marked Verified were tested at the store's checkout.
  • Always check the total at checkout before you pay.
