The short answer

BUR BUR is a real US company with a Shopify checkout, a published address and full policies. Its two best sellers score 4.8 out of 5 on its own store, from 413 reviews for the oil and 215 for the Mermaid Brush, but Trustpilot has only one review, too few to say much. Prices are premium, $56 for a 50 ml bottle of oil and $78 for a brush, and returns are limited to unopened items within 14 days.

What BUR BUR sells

Hair care built around burdock oil: the Growing Season hair oil, a shampoo and conditioner duo, a deep repair mask, a styling cream, and hair tools such as the Mermaid boar bristle and detangling brushes, a round brush, a brush cleaner and a derma roller, plus bundles of these. When we checked, prices ran from $26 for the brush cleaner to $166 for the One of a Kind bundle.

Its About page says the brand was started by two childhood friends, Anna and Ksenia, from a burdock hair mask recipe made by Ksenia's grandmother, Ludmila. BUR BUR describes its formulas as vegan and cruelty-free and says it's Leaping Bunny certified.

Is BUR BUR legit?

Yes, as far as we can see. The shop names its company, BUR BUR CARE LLC, with an address in Glendale, California, sells through a Shopify checkout, ships returns through a US warehouse in Rancho Cordova, California, and publishes shipping, refund and contact pages. Paying by card or PayPal gives you buyer protection if something goes wrong.

Is it worth the money?

It's a premium price for a hair oil: $56 for 50 ml, or $1.12 per ml. The bundles and the 15% subscription bring that down, and the Mermaid Brush at $78 sits in the same range as other handmade boar bristle brushes. Since opened products can't be returned, start with a single bottle or the brush rather than a large bundle.
