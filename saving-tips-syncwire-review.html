The short answer

Syncwire is a real accessory brand with a mixed record: 3.9 out of 5 on Trustpilot from 61 reviews, and 3.8 to 4.6 out of 5 for its best-selling grips on its own store. Prices are low, from $19.99 for a ring stand, and US shipping is free. The common complaint is the rubber face of the magnetic ring coming loose.

What Syncwire sells

The best sellers when we checked were all magnetic phone grips: the Ultra-Slim Magnetic Phone Ring Holder ($24.99 to $28.99), the Magnetic 360Grip in pastel and sparkly colors ($31.99), the Gym Magnetic Phone Holder for MagSafe ($29.99), the DuoGrip dual ring stand ($24.99) and the AirGrip single ring stand ($19.99). It also sells car mounts, cables and FM transmitters.

On Trustpilot, 41 of the 61 reviews give 5 stars and 15 give 1 star. Happy reviewers praise light, everyday grips and quick replies from support, with replacements sent for broken items.

Several reviews describe the same fault: the rubber cover on the magnetic ring coming off. Most of those say Syncwire replaced it, but a few 1-star reviews say a warranty claim went unanswered or the replacement was a different product.

Is Syncwire legit?

Yes, by what we could check. Its Trustpilot profile lists an address on Longguan Road in Shenzhen, China, with an email and phone number, and the store's account lists Kowloon, Hong Kong. The store publishes its shipping and refund policies, and many Trustpilot reviewers bought its products on Amazon.

Is it worth the money?

For a magnetic grip, the prices are fair, and the Ultra-Slim holder has the best rating of the range, 4.6 from 96 reviews. The 360Grip models score lower, 3.8. If the rubber face matters to you, keep the receipt and register a problem within the return window.
