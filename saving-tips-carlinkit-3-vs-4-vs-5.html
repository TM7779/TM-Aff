One naming trap: Carlinkit uses "5.0" for two different products. The 5.0 Mini Ultra is a small USB stick, and the 5.0 2Air is a box like the 4.0. Both are listed for wireless CarPlay and Android Auto.

Carlinkit 3.0: for iPhone-only households

The 3.0 Mini does one job: it turns a car's factory wired CarPlay into wireless CarPlay for an iPhone. Its product page says in capitals that it is not for Android Auto, and that the car must have factory wired CarPlay. Carlinkit says it weighs under 30 grams and usually connects in 7 to 14 seconds, depending on the phone and car.

At $38.00, or $34.96 with the code, it's the cheapest Carlinkit adapter. If nobody who drives the car uses an Android phone, the extra features of the 4.0 and 5.0 don't buy you anything. Carlinkit also sells 3.0 U2W Plus versions made for one car brand (Audi, Mercedes, Toyota and others) at $59.00.

Carlinkit 4.0: for CarPlay cars with Android phones too

The 4.0 (model CPC200-CP2A) is listed for cars with factory wired CarPlay, and its page says it suits families with both an Android phone and an iPhone, without switching or restarting. Carlinkit says it works with more than 98% of factory wired CarPlay cars, uses two antennas on 2.4 and 5.8 GHz Wi-Fi, saves up to 5 phones and supports touchpad cars. It comes with USB-A and USB-C cables.

That makes it the model to look at if your car has CarPlay but no Android Auto and someone drives with an Android phone. Ask Carlinkit's support to confirm your exact car before you order, since returns cost money (see below). It's $55.00, or $50.60 with the code.

Carlinkit 5.0: for cars with CarPlay or Android Auto

Both 5.0 models turn wired CarPlay or wired Android Auto into wireless: an iPhone gets wireless CarPlay and an Android phone gets wireless Android Auto. Their pages list cars with original wired CarPlay or wired Android Auto, iPhones on iOS 10 or later and Android 11 or later, 5.8 GHz Wi-Fi and online updates.

The 5.0 Mini Ultra is a USB stick that plugs straight into the car's port, with a USB-C adapter in the box, and costs $45.00 ($41.40 with the code). The 5.0 2Air is a small box with a cable, saves up to 5 phones and costs $69.33 ($63.78 with the code). On November 18, 2026 the Mini Ultra was $10.00 cheaper than the 4.0, so for a car that has wired Android Auto as well as CarPlay, the newer 5.0 is also the cheaper choice.

Questions people ask

Carlinkit 4.0 vs 5.0: what's the difference?

Both do wireless CarPlay and wireless Android Auto. Carlinkit lists the 4.0 for cars with factory wired CarPlay and phones of both kinds, and the 5.0 for cars with wired CarPlay or wired Android Auto. On November 18, 2026 the 4.0 was $55.00, the 5.0 Mini Ultra $45.00 and the 5.0 2Air $69.33.

Carlinkit 3.0 vs 4.0: which should I get?

The 3.0 Mini is wireless CarPlay for iPhones only, at $38.00. The 4.0 adds wireless Android Auto for Android phones and costs $55.00. If nobody uses an Android phone in the car, the 3.0 is enough.

Which Carlinkit is best?

There's no single best: it depends on your car and phones. For iPhones only, the 3.0 Mini is the cheapest. For a car with wired CarPlay and wired Android Auto, the 5.0 Mini Ultra covers both for $45.00.

Does the Carlinkit 3.0 work with Android Auto?

No. Its product page says it is not for Android Auto. It only turns factory wired CarPlay into wireless CarPlay for an iPhone.

Is the Carlinkit 5.0 the same as the 2Air?

The 2Air is one of two products Carlinkit sells as 5.0. The 5.0 2Air is a small box ($69.33); the 5.0 Mini Ultra is a USB stick ($45.00). Both do wireless CarPlay and Android Auto.

Will a Carlinkit adapter work if my car has no CarPlay?

No. Every Carlinkit adapter in this comparison needs a car with factory wired CarPlay, or wired Android Auto for the models that list it.

How long does a Carlinkit take to connect?

Carlinkit says the 3.0 Mini usually connects in 7 to 14 seconds, depending on the phone and car. After the first pairing, all of these models are listed to connect automatically when you start the car.

How many phones can a Carlinkit remember?

The 4.0 and 5.0 2Air pages say they save up to 5 phones and connect to them automatically. The 3.0 Mini and 5.0 Mini Ultra pages don't give a number.
