The short answer

Mellif is a real online tool store whose products run on other brands' batteries. Its product ratings look good, but it has no Trustpilot page to compare them with, and its policy allows up to 30 days for shipping on top of processing.

What Mellif sells

Cordless tools and outdoor gear built to fit DeWalt, Makita, Milwaukee and similar batteries: snow shovels, air dusters, tire inflators, stick pumps, hot glue guns and soldering stations, plus battery kits.

When we checked, the hot glue gun was $29.99, the air duster $59.99 and the 20-inch snow shovel $299.99 without a battery.

Is Mellif legit?

It looks like a working store with written shipping and return policies, an About page and a support email. The pages we checked don't name a company address, and the long shipping window is the main thing to accept before you order.

Is it worth the money?

If you already own DeWalt, Makita or Milwaukee batteries, buying a tool without a battery can cost much less than starting a new system. It's less attractive if you need the tool soon, because of the delivery time.
