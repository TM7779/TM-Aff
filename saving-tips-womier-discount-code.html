Shipping and returns

In-stock items ship within 2 to 3 business days. Standard shipping takes 8 to 15 business days, and DHL Express (3 to 7 days) costs $19.99 or more extra in some countries.

For a change of mind, ask for a return within 7 days of delivery; the keyboard must be unused and in its original packaging, and you pay return postage. Faulty or wrong items reported within 7 days are replaced or refunded, and a 1-year limited warranty covers defects after that.
