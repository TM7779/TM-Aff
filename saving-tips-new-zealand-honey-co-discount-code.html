New Zealand Honey Co. review: the short answer

New Zealand Honey Co. is a real company with a published address in New Zealand, US and NZ phone numbers, written shipping and refund policies and thousands of reviews on its own store. Its honey carries the independent UMF grade, and the batch number on each jar links to that batch's lab results on its site. The catch is the price of the higher grades and a return policy that excludes opened honey.

What New Zealand Honey Co. sells

Mostly raw Manuka honey in seven UMF grades, from UMF 10+ (MGO 263+) at $18.88 for 250g to UMF 30+ (MGO 1620+) at $600 for 250g. Most grades come in 125g, 250g and 500g jars, a 500g squeezy bottle and boxes of 10 single-serve packets. It also sells honey drops, a throat spray with propolis, a Manuka honey for pets, a wooden spoon ($3.99) and kits that bundle a jar with packets and drops.

The brand says its honey is cold-extracted and unpasteurised, and certified non-GMO, glyphosate-free and halal. Those are the brand's statements; the UMF grade is the part checked by an outside body. Like all honey, it isn't suitable for babies under 12 months.

Is New Zealand Honey Co. legit?

Yes. Its contact page lists a street address at 75 Maunganui Road in Mount Maunganui, a New Zealand and a US phone number, and its products carry the UMF quality mark, which is licensed by New Zealand's UMF Honey Association. Each jar has a lot number you can enter on its site to see that batch's test results. Orders are tracked, and US orders ship from a US warehouse in 1 to 3 business days, according to its shipping policy.

Is New Zealand Honey Co. worth the money?

It depends on the grade. UMF 10+ at $18.88 for 250g is priced like an everyday Manuka honey; UMF 24+ and above cost several times more per jar for a higher measured MGO. If you want Manuka honey for eating, the lower grades and the 500g jars give you the most honey for the money. The Black Friday sale (up to 30% off until December 1, 2026) and Subscribe & Save (20% to 25%) cut the price more than most codes.
