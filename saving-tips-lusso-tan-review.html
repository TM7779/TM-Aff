The short answer

Lusso Tan is a real UK business that sells through its own store, with written shipping and return policies. Its tanners were on sale for about a third off when we checked, and orders over £35 ship free. There's almost no independent review data yet, so start with one mousse.

What Lusso Tan sells

Self-tanning mousses (Rapid Tan Mousse, and a Tinted Self Tanning Mousse in Medium, Dark and Ultra Dark), tanning balms (Winter Nights and Summer Meadow), The Daily Gradual Tanner, a PH Balancing Primer, tanning and exfoliating mitts, and tan removers like a bath bomb, a shower bar and a shower gel.

Most 200ml tanners cost £14 to £22.40 on sale. Lusso Tan says its formulas are vegan and made without parabens or alcohol.

Is Lusso Tan legit?

Yes. Its store is registered in Holywood, Northern Ireland, and it ships to the UK, Ireland, Europe, the USA, Canada, Australia and New Zealand, aiming to dispatch within 3 days. Its return policy gives you 14 days to cancel, though opened tanning products can't be returned.

Is it worth the money?

At £15.60 for a 200ml mousse on sale, it's priced like a mid-range self-tanner. The balms cost more, at £22.40. Because opened products can't be returned, try one bottle before buying a bundle.
