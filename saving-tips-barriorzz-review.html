The short answer

BARRIORZZ is a real small Dutch calisthenics brand with very high store ratings for its dip belt. It has few reviews overall, 4.3 on Trustpilot, and a 14-day return window with return shipping at your cost.

What BARRIORZZ sells

Dip belts, gymnastic rings, parallettes, wrist wraps, chalk, resistance bands, battle ropes and apparel for calisthenics and streetlifting.

When we checked, the Dip Belt Pro was $57 in black, the Athlete Ring Set $48 and Wrist Wraps $23 (was $26).

Is BARRIORZZ legit?

Yes. It says it began in 2014 at a calisthenics park in Heerlen, the Netherlands, registered as a company in 2019 and launched its store in 2023. It lists a Heerlen return address.
