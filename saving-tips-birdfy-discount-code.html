Shipping and returns

Birdfy ships to the contiguous US (not Alaska, Hawaii, Puerto Rico, PO boxes or APO addresses), Canada, Australia, New Zealand and parts of Asia. US standard shipping takes 3 to 7 business days and is free from $59.99; express takes 1 to 3 business days. Canada takes 10 to 15 business days, and Australia and New Zealand 15 to 20, with shipping worked out at checkout. UK and European shoppers are sent to Birdfy's regional stores.

Birdfy's site advertises a 60-day free return. To return something, email support@birdfy.com first; once the return is approved, ship it back within 10 days with all cords, accessories and the original box. You pay return shipping if you simply changed your mind; Birdfy sends a label for a defective product. Refunds are processed 3 to 5 business days after you send the tracking number. Products carry a two-year warranty for the original buyer.
