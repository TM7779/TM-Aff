The short answer

Omega is an established juicer brand owned by The Legacy Companies (Greenfield World Trade, Inc.), with written shipping, return and warranty terms. Its store shows ratings from 4.1 to 4.5 out of 5 on its main juicers. The biggest thing to know before you buy: the Effortless and Pure Press list a 15-year limited warranty, while the cheaper JC4000, Time Saving batch juicer and High Speed juicer list only 1 year.

What Omega sells

On November 14, 2026, omegajuicers.com listed five juicers: the High Speed Juicer with Extra Large Chute ($109.99, a 1,000-watt centrifugal model), the JC4000 Wide Mouth Vertical Cold Press Juicer ($134.99, 50 to 75 RPM, 3-inch mouth), the Time Saving Cold Press Batch Juicer ($159.99, down from $199.99), the Pure Press VSJ843 vertical slow juicer ($399.99) and the Effortless Batch Juicer with a 2-liter hopper ($499.99 to $549.99).

The rest of the store, about 129 of its 134 products, is spare parts and accessories for current and older models such as the J8006HDS: augers, screens, drums, end caps and brushes. The J8006 juicer itself wasn't listed for sale that day, only its parts.

Is Omega legit?

Yes. omegajuicers.com is run by Greenfield World Trade, Inc., doing business as The Legacy Companies, which also owns Avanti Products and Edgecraft; Omega Products, Inc. is one of its companies. The store publishes its shipping policy, a 30-day return policy for US and Canadian buyers, instruction manuals for more than 20 models and a warranty claim page.

Customer service works through live chat and a contact form; the store doesn't list a phone number or street address on its contact pages. Returns need an RMA number from that form before you send anything back, and you pay the return shipping.

Is it worth the money?

For daily juicing, the Pure Press and the Effortless are the models to look at, mainly because of their 15-year warranty: over that time the $399.99 Pure Press works out to about $27 a year. The JC4000 is the cheapest Omega slow juicer at $134.99, but it's covered for 12 months, so it suits someone trying out juicing rather than someone replacing a machine for the long run.

If you mostly juice soft fruit and want speed, the $109.99 High Speed juicer does the job for the least money. Our Omega juicer discount code guide lists every way to pay less, with prices side by side.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • 15-year limited warranty on the Effortless and Pure Press
  • Batch juicers that take a whole recipe at once
  • Spare parts for older models sold on the same store
  • Instruction manuals for more than 20 models online
  • Ratings of 4.1 to 4.5 stars on the main juicers

Cons

  • Only 1 year of warranty on the JC4000, Time Saving and High Speed models
  • Shipping is charged at UPS rates outside of sales
  • Returns need an RMA first, and you pay the return shipping
  • No phone number on the contact pages; support is chat and form only
  • A smaller range than before: the J8006 juicer wasn't listed, only its parts

Who it's for

People who want a slow juicer from a long-standing brand with parts and manuals available, and who value a long warranty. Busy households that want to load a whole recipe at once will like the batch models. Beginners on a budget can start with the JC4000 or the High Speed juicer, knowing the warranty is short.
