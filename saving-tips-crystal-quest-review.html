The short answer

Crystal Quest is a real US company with a published business address and phone number, and its best sellers are rated 4.8 to 4.9 out of 5 on its own store. The prices are fair for small filters, from $39.95 for a pitcher. The catch is the return policy: you pay return shipping, and a restocking fee of up to 30% can apply, so it suits buyers who know which filter they need.

What Crystal Quest sells

The range runs from small to very large. At the small end are the Water Pitcher Filter System ($39.95 to $42.95), the Shower Filter ($40.95 to $44.95) and the Bath Ball Filter (from $64.95). In the middle are the SMART countertop filters (from $86.90) and under-sink filters ($149.90 to $208.80), and reverse osmosis under-sink systems from $298.40. At the top are whole-house filters and softeners from about $1,300 to over $10,000.

Many systems come in versions named after what the cartridge targets, such as fluoride, arsenic, lead, nitrate or alkaline. Crystal Quest also sells replacement cartridges, with a subscription, and city and well water tests from $159. Prices checked on November 19, 2026.

The weak point we found is in the rules rather than the ratings: the refund policy itself warns buyers to know their water before ordering, because returns cost shipping and a restocking fee.

Is Crystal Quest legit?

Yes, by what we could check. The store lists its business as Quest Technologies, Inc., 175 Chastain Meadows Ct NW, Kennesaw, Georgia, with a toll-free phone number. Its About page says it assembles its products in the USA from US-designed parts and parts from domestic and global suppliers, and that it has an A+ BBB rating; those are the company's own words.

Its product pages state certifications for the filter media; for the pitcher, it says the medium is certified to Standard 42 (aesthetic effects) and by the WQA to Standard 61. The store shows its shipping, refund and contact policies in full.

Is it worth the money?

For the small filters, the prices are in line with other brands: $39.95 for a pitcher, $18 for a pitcher cartridge that Crystal Quest says lasts 2,000 gallons or 6 to 12 months, and $40.95 for a shower filter. The SMART under-sink filter at $149.90 is rated by Crystal Quest for about 10,000 gallons, or 1 to 3 years.

The bigger systems are a bigger decision, and the return rules matter more there: a restocking fee of up to 30% on a $2,000 system is a lot of money. If you aren't sure what your water needs, a water test first is the safer order.

Who it's for

Crystal Quest suits people who want a filter pitcher, a shower filter or an under-sink system from a US company, and homeowners who already know what is in their water and want a system matched to it. It's a poor fit if you want to try a filter and send it back freely, or if you aren't sure which system you need yet.
