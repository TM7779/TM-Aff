The short answer

Genuine Essiac is a legitimate US seller with good ratings, free US shipping and long-lasting bags. Buy it as a herbal tea you enjoy, not as a treatment: there's no good clinical evidence that Essiac treats any disease.

What Genuine Essiac sells

Powdered Essiac tea made from eight herbs: blessed thistle, burdock root, kelp, red clover, sheep sorrel (with the root), slippery elm bark, Turkish rhubarb root and watercress.

A 1 lb bag is $69.97 and lasts about two months for one adult, according to the brand.
