Shipping and returns

Simpletics aims to dispatch orders within 1 to 3 business days and emails a tracking number once an order ships; it says delays can happen at busy times. Several Trustpilot reviewers outside the US wrote about long waits and extra delivery charges on arrival, so international buyers should expect possible import fees.

Returns are covered by a 30-day money-back guarantee: email support within 30 days of delivery with your order number, and it applies to opened and used products too. Don't send anything back before contacting them; if they want the item, they email a prepaid label. Approved refunds go to the original payment method within 10 business days.
