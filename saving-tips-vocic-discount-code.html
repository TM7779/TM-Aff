Shipping and returns

Shipping is free to the US mainland; VOCIC doesn't ship to Alaska, Hawaii, Rhode Island, Guam or Puerto Rico. Most orders ship in 1 to 3 business days and delivery takes 7 to 15 business days.

Items can be returned within 30 days, undamaged and in the original packaging; email support@vocic.com with the subject "Order Return Request", your order number and photos. VOCIC pays for returns over quality problems; for any other reason, you pay return shipping.
