2. Look at the two big collections for long trips

Shaka Guide sells two larger collections, both marked as Black Friday deals when we checked:

  • The Hawaii Tour Collection: all 21 Hawaii tours for $59.99 (shown as $79.99 before the sale). It suits a trip to two or more islands.
  • The Ultimate National Park Tour Collection: 55 tours for $114.99 (shown as $199.99 before the sale). National park tours cost $14.99 to $22.99 each, so it pays off from about seven parks.

The Black Friday page says the offer can't be combined with other promotions, so expect the code not to take more off these two collections while the sale runs.

3. Buy on the website, not through an app store

Shaka Guide's own sale terms say its website discounts don't apply to App Store, Google Play, Viator, Go City and other resellers. Buy on shakaguide.com, check out with the code, then open the receipt email on your phone: it has a download link and a redeem code (it starts with SG_) that unlocks the tours under My Stuff in the app.

Refunds and what you get

Tours are digital, so there's nothing to ship. Shaka Guide's terms say payments are non-refundable except as the terms provide, and that refunds under its money-back satisfaction guarantee are at its discretion. Contact its support (by phone, chat or email from the support page) before disputing a charge.

Each tour includes offline audio, an offline map, turn-by-turn directions and a list of stops and nearby sights. Download it over Wi-Fi before you set out: after that it works without cell service. The app works with Apple CarPlay but not Android Auto, according to Shaka Guide's FAQ. The Black Friday page also says tours never expire.
