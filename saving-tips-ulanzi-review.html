The short answer

Ulanzi is a real, established brand. Its store lists a business address in Wan Chai, Hong Kong, its About page traces sales back to 2015, and it publishes shipping, return and warranty policies. Its best sellers are rated 4.7 to 4.9 out of 5 on its own product pages, and most of them cost $24 to $60. The two things to know before you buy: you have only 14 days to ask for a return and you pay return shipping unless the item is faulty, and some items ship from overseas in 10 to 12 business days.

Is Ulanzi legit?

Yes. Ulanzi's About page says it led AliExpress's camera and photo category from 2015 to 2018, was sold in 70 countries by 2020, raised Series A+ funding in 2022, and now sells in more than 190 countries and regions with over 3,400 products and 600 patents. Those numbers come from Ulanzi itself. Its store gives a business address in Wan Chai, Hong Kong, a service email, and support hours of 9:00 to 18:00 China time, Monday to Friday.

People also search for Ulanzi on Amazon and AliExpress. Ulanzi sells there too, but the code and the 4-year warranty on travel tripods apply only to orders on ulanzi.com.

Is it worth the money?

For phone and small-camera shooters, the prices are low for what you get: the MT-44 tripod is $25.95, the MA30 phone tripod $24.99 and the VL49 RGB light $29.95. The F38 quick release kit is $29.95, a common way to start with quick release plates. At the top end, the Zero Y travel tripod is $279.95 and the Falcam TreeRoot $387, priced against other carbon fiber travel tripods.

The trade-offs are the return window and shipping. You have 14 days to ask for a return, you pay return shipping unless the item is faulty, and items that aren't stocked in the US or Germany take 10 to 12 business days. Check the model list on a mount or cage page before you buy, so it fits your phone or camera.

Who it's for

Ulanzi suits phone vloggers, travelers and hobby photographers who want a light tripod, a small light or a quick release system without paying premium-brand prices, and creators who already use F38 plates. It's a poor fit if you want a long time to decide on a return, or if you need an item next week that isn't stocked in the US or Germany.
