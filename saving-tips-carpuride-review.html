The short answer

Carpuride is a real company with a published Hong Kong address, phone number and email, free shipping, a 30-day return window and a 2-year warranty listed on its product pages. Its own store shows high ratings, such as 4.82 out of 5 from 2,148 reviews on the W702BS. Two catches: returns need email approval first, and the store's review widget shows almost no ratings below 4 stars, so it's worth reading independent owner reviews before a $300-plus purchase.

What Carpuride sells

Most of the range is motorcycle screens: the W502 Pro and W702 Pro (5 and 7 inches, $319.99 and $339.99), the 702RS Pro with a radar blind spot sensor ($439.99), the W702 Plus that can install apps ($409.99), and the W502BS, W602BS and W702BS made for BMW motorcycles with the Wonder Wheel controller ($409.90 to $449.99). Several come with optional tire pressure sensors.

For cars there are portable dashboard screens such as the C3 ($159.99) and C92 ($169.99), and the 10.25-inch W125A and W125S with built-in dash cams ($299.90 and $319.90). The store also sells mounts, brackets and cables.

Every product page warns that the screens aren't compatible with HarmonyOS phones and some Android models.

Is Carpuride legit?

Yes, in the sense that it's an established brand selling real products with written policies. Its contact page lists an email, a Hong Kong phone number and an address at 148 Electric Road, North Point, Hong Kong. Orders ship by DHL, UPS or DPD with a tracking number.

The return policy is stricter than many US stores: you must get a return authorization by email before sending anything back, and opened or used items without a fault may be refused. Keep all packaging until you're sure the screen works with your phone and bike.

Is it worth the money?

If your bike or car has no CarPlay, a Carpuride screen costs far less than a new head unit or a dedicated motorcycle GPS, and with code USABLEFISH the popular W702 Pro drops from $339.99 to $237.99. The BMW models make most sense if you want the bike's Wonder Wheel to control the screen. If you only need navigation in a car, the C3 at $159.99 does the basic job for less.
