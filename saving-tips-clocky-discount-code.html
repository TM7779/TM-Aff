Shipping and returns

Shipping is free on US orders. Orders take 1 to 2 business days to process, then 3 to 5 business days by UPS Ground. Orders outside the US take 2 to 3 weeks, and you may pay import duties; the FAQ points buyers abroad to Clocky's own stores on Amazon in Canada, the UK, Germany, France, Japan, Australia and other countries.

The return policy asks you to email team@clocky.com for approval first and send the item back in its original packaging within 15 days of receipt (the FAQ says 30 days, so ask when you email). You pay the return shipping, shipping charges aren't refunded, and refunds are the purchase price minus a $6 restocking fee.
