Shipping and returns

Orders are confirmed within 48 hours and processed in 2 to 4 days, not counting weekends. Delivery takes 7 to 12 business days, pre-sale items excluded. JSAUX doesn't ship to APO/FPO addresses or some islands, and import duties outside the US are the buyer's to pay.

Unused, undamaged items can be returned within 15 days of delivery in their original packaging. You pay return shipping unless the item is faulty; email official@jsaux.com first for the return address. Refunds go back to the original payment method.
