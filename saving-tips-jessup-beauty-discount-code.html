Shipping and returns

Orders are dispatched within 2 business days with tracking. Delivery usually takes 3 to 20 business days depending on the country; selected items ship from a US warehouse in about 2 to 5 business days.

Unused items in their original packaging can be returned within 90 days of purchase. Sale items can't be refunded, and defective or damaged items are replaced. Brushes carry up to 6 months of quality cover.
