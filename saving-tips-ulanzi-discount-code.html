1. Spend $79 for free shipping

Ulanzi's shipping policy says standard shipping costs $5.99 and is free on orders over $79. If you're at $70, a $23.95 MT-16 tripod or a $29.95 light can cost little more than the shipping fee you'd otherwise pay.

Shipping and returns

Ulanzi aims to process orders in 1 to 2 business days, longer during big sales. Items in stock in the US or Germany arrive in 3 to 4 business days; other items take 10 to 12 business days. Both cost $5.99, or nothing over $79. Ulanzi uses carriers such as USPS, UPS, DHL and YunExpress, and emails a tracking number when the order ships.

You can ask for a return within 14 days of receiving a product. It must be unused, in the same condition and in its original packaging, with proof of purchase. Email Ulanzi's service team for the return address first: the policy says returns sent to another address can't be refunded. Unless the product is faulty, you pay the return shipping; there's no restocking fee. Orders can be canceled for a full refund until they ship.
