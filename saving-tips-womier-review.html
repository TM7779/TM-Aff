The short answer

Womier is a real keyboard brand with a US company address in Irvine, California, and low prices. Buyers often like the keyboards themselves, but its Trustpilot page scores 2.6 out of 5, mostly over customer support, and its change-of-mind return window is only 7 days.

What Womier sells

Keyboards from 30% to full size: Hall effect (magnetic) and TMR models such as the SK61 HE and WD75 HE, wireless mechanical boards like the Q61 Pro, wooden and aluminum cases, plus switches, keycaps, desk mats and cables.

When we checked, keyboards ran from $39.99 for the wired WK61 V2 to about $125 for the WD75 HE, on sale.

Positive Trustpilot reviews praise the look, sound and setup of the keyboards. The one-star reviews are almost all about support: unanswered warranty emails, a keyboard that arrived dead in thin packaging, and help after a firmware update. Womier has claimed its Trustpilot page. Store reviews are few on each product.

Is Womier legit?

Yes. Its store lists WOMIER INC in Irvine, California, with a phone number and email, it ships with tracking to many countries, and it publishes a return policy and a 1-year limited warranty.

Is it worth the money?

For the price, Womier's Hall effect boards are among the cheapest ways to try magnetic switches. The trade-off is the short return window and the support complaints, so check your keyboard as soon as it arrives.

Who it's for

Gamers and keyboard hobbyists who want a Hall effect or custom-feel keyboard on a budget and are comfortable troubleshooting firmware themselves. If you'd want quick, hands-on support, a brand with a local service center may suit better.
