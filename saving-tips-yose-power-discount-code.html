Shipping and returns

Yose Power says shipping is free across Europe. Batteries and kits leave its Berlin warehouse within 1 to 2 working days; delivery takes 2 to 4 working days in Germany and longer elsewhere in Europe. Some accessories ship from warehouses outside Germany. Every parcel has tracking.

You can return a new, unused product in its original packaging within 30 days, with proof of purchase. Email service@yosepower.com for the return address first. You pay return shipping unless the item was wrong or damaged, and sale items can't be returned. Refunds are made within 48 working hours of the return being confirmed, Yose Power says.
