The short answer

ChicChoi is a real shop with a Shopify checkout, published policies and contact details. Its best sellers score 4.8 to 5 out of 5 on its own store, from 12 to 69 reviews each, but we found no Trustpilot page to compare. Orders ship from China and take one to three weeks to the US, and a change-of-mind return costs you postage plus a 10% restocking fee.

What ChicChoi sells

Drawstring backpacks and bags, pencil cases, planners and planner inserts, loose-leaf notebooks, travel journals, calendars, gel pens, stamps, washi stickers and other journaling supplies, many with its own illustrated designs. When we checked, its best sellers cost from $7.90 for a set of gel pens to $42.90 for the DOT DOT backpack.

Its About page says it's run by a team of women who love planning and journaling, with the motto "Cute Stationery Motivates".

Is ChicChoi legit?

Yes, as far as we can see. It names its company, CHICCHOI LIMITED, lists a WhatsApp number, a Telegram account and a support email, sells through a Shopify checkout with PayPal, cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay, Klarna and Afterpay, gives tracking for every parcel, and publishes full shipping and refund policies. Paying by card or PayPal gives you buyer protection if something goes wrong.

Is it worth the money?

If you like its designs, prices are in line with other cute stationery shops: $19.80 for a large pencil case and about $40 for a drawstring backpack, with US, UK and EU import duties paid by ChicChoi. Plan around 2 to 3 weeks for delivery to the US, and buy only what you're sure about, since change-of-mind returns cost postage to China and 10%.
