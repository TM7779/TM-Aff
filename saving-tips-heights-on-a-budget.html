Three ways not to waste money

  • Don't double up. Check the labels of what you already take before adding something new.
  • Give it time before switching. Heights suggests at least three months for Vitals⁺, Biotic⁺ and Thrive⁺, and two for Magnesium⁺. Judging after a week often means buying something else you didn't need.
  • Use the money-back guarantee. Heights gives you 30 days to return a product that isn't right for you, which beats leaving a half-used jar in the cupboard.

Before you start

Supplements add to a varied diet; they don't replace one. If you're pregnant, breastfeeding, taking medication or managing a health condition, check with your doctor or pharmacist first, and don't take more than the dose on the label.
