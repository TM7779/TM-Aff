Shipping and returns

Orders are sent from Telesin's fulfillment center within 1 to 3 business days. The shipping policy says standard shipping usually takes 2 to 4 weeks, international delivery about 7 to 20 working days, and express shipping about 7 to 15 days for $25 to $35. Busy periods such as Black Friday and Christmas can take longer.

Telesin has a 30-day return policy: you have 30 days after receiving an item to request a return, and it must be unused, with tags and original packaging, plus proof of purchase. Ask first by email; items sent back without an approved return aren't accepted. If a return is accepted, Telesin sends a return shipping label. The policy also says it can't accept returns on sale items or gift cards. Since most products showed a sale price on November 18, 2026, ask support before you buy if being able to return the item matters to you. Defective, damaged or wrong items should be reported right away.
