MaryRuth's is a legitimate, widely sold brand whose best seller, the Liquid Morning Multivitamin, rates 4.6 stars from over 6,000 reviews. It's a good pick if you want a liquid or gummy format. Know that its guarantee gives an exchange or store credit, not a cash refund.

Vegan, non-GMO and gluten-free vitamins in liquid and gummy form: the Liquid Morning Multivitamin, a Hair Growth version, kids' gummies, liquid iron and more.

Is it worth the money?

The 32 fl oz bottle at $42.95 costs less with a 10% subscription and less again with a code. Standard shipping is free over $49. It's worth it if you struggle with pills; if capsules are fine for you, cheaper tablet multivitamins exist.
