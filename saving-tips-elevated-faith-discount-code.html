3. Use Faith Rewards points on a later order

Faith Rewards is Elevated Faith's free loyalty program. You earn points on purchases, verified reviews, referrals and your birthday month, then turn them into a discount code. A rewards code can't be combined with JWickenden15, so use whichever saves more on that order.

Shipping and returns

Orders are processed within 3 business days, and most leave in under 48 hours. You can pick Expedited Shipping for 2-day air. Once an order is placed, it can't be cancelled or changed, including the address.

Unworn, unwashed or defective items can be returned or exchanged within 35 days of the shipping date. Exchanges are free, while refunds carry a $7 handling fee. Phone cases, stickers, rings, socks, wristbands, string bracelets, keychains, mystery items and final-sale items can't be returned.
