The short answer

EPOMAKER is a real keyboard brand that started on a crowdfunding platform in 2019 and scores 4.2 out of 5 from 149 reviews on Trustpilot. Buyers mostly praise the sound and build; the complaints are about battery life, patchy wireless on some models and slow standard shipping.

What EPOMAKER sells

Keyboards in many layouts: the wooden Bamboo75, the Galaxy75, the TH and RT lines, and Hall effect or TMR (magnetic switch) boards such as the HE60 and HE65 V2 TMR. It also sells switches, keycaps, mice and audio gear.

When we checked, best sellers ran from $59.99 for the HE60 and the HE65 V2 TMR (on sale from $99.99) to $109.99 for the Bamboo75.

Is EPOMAKER legit?

Yes. It has sold keyboards since a 2019 crowdfunding launch, publishes shipping, return, warranty and price-match policies, answers at support@epomaker.com, and ships with tracking to most countries. Its contact page lists a trade name and email but no street address.

Is it worth the money?

For a magnetic-switch or wooden keyboard under about $110, EPOMAKER is good value, and the 30-day return and 1-year warranty lower the risk. Ask for a refund to your card if you return something, because the default is store credit.
