The short answer

VANKYO is a real brand with a working store, published policies, a US phone line and a US warehouse address. Its projectors are inexpensive for what they list: native 1080p, Roku TV or Google TV, and auto focus on the mid-range models for $199.99 to $299.99. ivankyo.com doesn't show customer ratings, and we couldn't load Trustpilot, so we can't give you a star score; this review sticks to what the store publishes.

What VANKYO sells

On November 16, 2026, ivankyo.com listed 17 products: projectors from $69.99 to $429.99, a 4K ultra-short-throw laser projector (Vista T4, $1,999.99), and accessories such as a 120" portable screen, a tripod stand, a ceiling mount and replacement remotes.

Is VANKYO legit?

Yes, it's a real company selling real products. The site's footer says ivankyo.com is operated by SHARPVISION TECH CO., LIMITED in Hong Kong; the contact page lists a headquarters in Shenzhen, China, a US warehouse in Beltsville, Maryland, the support email support@ivankyo.com and a phone line, 1-800-531-0915, open Monday to Friday, 9 a.m. to 5 p.m. Eastern.

The store publishes full shipping, return and warranty policies (updated July 29, 2026), offers 30-day returns including change of mind, and gives projectors a one-year warranty. Its policies note that products bought from Amazon, Walmart or other sellers must go back through that seller.

Is it worth the money?

For a first projector, a kids' room or casual movie nights, VANKYO's prices are low for native 1080p with a streaming system built in: the H500R is $199.99 and needs no streaming stick. The trade-off is brightness. At 150 to 400 lumens, most models need a dim or dark room; only the V700G Pro (730 lumens) is pitched for brighter rooms and outdoor use.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Native 1080p with Roku TV or Google TV from $199.99
  • Free standard shipping to the contiguous US
  • 30-day returns, including change of mind
  • One-year warranty on projectors
  • US phone support and a US warehouse

Cons

  • Most models are 150 to 400 lumens, so they need a dark room
  • No customer ratings on the brand's own site
  • You pay return shipping on change-of-mind returns
  • No shipping to Alaska, Hawaii, Puerto Rico or military addresses
  • The H500R has a single HDMI 1.4 port

Who it's for

VANKYO suits budget buyers who want a big picture in a bedroom, dorm or basement, families setting up movie nights, and anyone who wants Roku TV or Google TV built in rather than a separate streaming stick. It's a poor fit for bright living rooms, gamers who need several HDMI inputs, and buyers outside the contiguous US.

Questions people ask

Is VANKYO legit?

Yes. ivankyo.com is run by SHARPVISION TECH CO., LIMITED, lists a US warehouse in Beltsville, Maryland and a support line (1-800-531-0915), and publishes full shipping, return and warranty policies.

Is VANKYO a good projector brand?

VANKYO sells low-priced projectors with native 1080p and Roku TV or Google TV on its mid-range models. Most are 150 to 400 lumens, so they work best in dim rooms; the V700G Pro is the brightest at 730 lumens.

Is a VANKYO projector worth it?

For dark rooms and casual viewing, yes: the H500R is $199.99 with Roku TV built in. For a bright room with the lights on, look at a brighter projector.

What is VANKYO's Trustpilot rating?

We couldn't load VANKYO's Trustpilot page on November 16, 2026, so we can't quote a score. ivankyo.com doesn't show ratings on its product pages either.

How bright is the VANKYO H500R?

VANKYO lists the Performance H500R at 330 ANSI lumens, with native 1920 × 1080 resolution, a 40" to 150" picture and auto focus and keystone.

What is the difference between the VANKYO V700 Neo and the V700G Pro?

Both are native 1080p Google TV projectors with two 8W speakers. The V700 Neo is 400 ANSI lumens for $299.99; the V700G Pro is 730 ANSI lumens, adds obstacle avoidance and screen alignment, and comes with a 120" screen for $429.99.

Who makes VANKYO projectors?

The store's footer says ivankyo.com is operated by SHARPVISION TECH CO., LIMITED in Hong Kong. The contact page lists a headquarters in Shenzhen, China, and a US warehouse in Maryland.

What warranty does VANKYO have?

Projectors come with a one-year warranty from the original purchase date. A repair or replacement doesn't restart the warranty.

What is VANKYO's return policy?

You can return eligible products within 30 calendar days of delivery after contacting customer support. VANKYO pays return shipping for confirmed defects; you pay it for change-of-mind returns.

Where does VANKYO ship?

Free standard shipping to the contiguous United States. It doesn't ship to Alaska, Hawaii, Puerto Rico, US territories or APO, FPO and DPO addresses.
