How to use the Lumibricks discount code

  1. Select Show code above. The code is copied for you and Lumibricks opens.
  2. Add what you want to your cart. Shipping is free from Lumibricks' US and UK warehouses.
  3. Paste the code at checkout. Find the discount code box, paste ZJZVQYLE and select Apply.
  4. Check the total before you pay. The discount shows as its own line in the order summary.

1. Use the code on a bigger set

The code takes 10% off the whole order, so it saves most on the large sets: $13 on the Izakaya or the Game Stack, and $17 on the 2,507-piece Apartment. The lights come with the set: the Izakaya lists 20 light points and 8 light strips, the Game Stack 23 light points and 10 strips.

2. Collect reward points

Lumibricks gives 100 points for creating an account. Points can be swapped for items in its rewards shop, which listed things like a USB hub, a building block organizer and a minifigure blind box when we checked. Its newsletter sign-up also offers 10% off; most stores take one code per order, so compare that with ZJZVQYLE at checkout.

3. Order from the warehouse closest to you

Lumibricks ships from warehouses in the US, Canada, Germany, the UK and China, and you pick your region on the product page. Shipping is free within the US, the UK, and to Germany, Czechia and Poland. Other EU countries pay $5.99 to $36, Canada $9.99, and Australia and the rest of the world pay what checkout shows, plus any import duties.

Shipping and returns

Lumibricks lists 3 to 5 business days from its US, Canada and UK warehouses, 3 to 6 within the EU, and 6 to 12 from China to Australia and other countries. It doesn't ship to PO boxes, APO addresses or parcel lockers.

You can return items within 30 days of delivery: outside the EU they must be unused, in original condition and packaging. EU buyers have a 14-day right of withdrawal. Submit the request through Lumibricks' form or email service@lumibricks.com; it says it replies within 2 business days and refunds within 5 to 10 business days after the item arrives. You pay return shipping unless the item is faulty or wrong.

If the Lumibricks code doesn't work

  • Check the spelling. Type ZJZVQYLE exactly, with no spaces before or after.
  • Remove other codes. Most stores accept one discount code per order.
  • Compare with sale prices. Items that are already discounted may not take an extra code. If the code isn't accepted, compare the totals and keep the lower one.
