Its newsletter sign-up also offers 10% off; most stores take one code per order, so compare that with ZJZVQYLE at checkout.

3. Order from the warehouse closest to you

Lumibricks ships from warehouses in the US, Canada, Germany, the UK and China, and you pick your region on the product page. Shipping is free within the US, the UK, and to Germany, Czechia and Poland. Other EU countries pay $5.99 to $36, Canada $9.99, and Australia and the rest of the world pay what checkout shows, plus any import duties.

Shipping and returns

Lumibricks lists 3 to 5 business days from its US, Canada and UK warehouses, 3 to 6 within the EU, and 6 to 12 from China to Australia and other countries. It doesn't ship to PO boxes, APO addresses or parcel lockers.

You can return items within 30 days of delivery: outside the EU they must be unused, in original condition and packaging. EU buyers have a 14-day right of withdrawal. Submit the request through Lumibricks' form or email service@lumibricks.com; it says it replies within 2 business days and refunds within 5 to 10 business days after the item arrives. You pay return shipping unless the item is faulty or wrong.

Lumibricks review: the short answer

Lumibricks is a real company. Its contact page names Hongkong SusenGo Technology Ltd. with an address in Guangzhou, China, and it ships from its own warehouses in the US, Canada, Germany and the UK. On Trustpilot it held 4.2 out of 5 from 165 reviews when we checked (its own site banner says 4.7). Buyers love how the finished sets look; the complaints are about missing or broken pieces and slow replies from support.

What Lumibricks sells

Display sets for adults, with the lighting designed into the build rather than added later: city shops (Book-Cafe, Record Store, Gelato Store, Newsstand), a cyberpunk "Neoncity" line (Izakaya, Game Stack), cabins and cottages (Aurora Cabin), Western scenes (Western Saloon, Western Freight Train), an Apartment and more. The best sellers run from $34.99 to $179.99 and from about 1,500 to 2,600 pieces, with recommended ages of 16+. Lumibricks says its prints are printed on the bricks, with no stickers.

Is Lumibricks legit?

Yes. lumibricks.com names the company and its address, gives a UK VAT number, publishes its shipping and return policies, and answers at service@lumibricks.com. Orders in the US, Canada, the UK and the EU ship from local warehouses in 3 to 6 business days, which matches what reviewers describe. One thing to note: the 4.7 rating on its banner is higher than the 4.2 Trustpilot showed when we looked.

Is Lumibricks worth the money?

The lighting is included in the price: the $129.99 Izakaya comes with 20 light points and 8 light strips, so there's no separate light kit to buy. Shipping is free in the US and UK. The risk is a missing or damaged piece, so open and check the bags soon after delivery while you're inside the 30-day return window.
