The short answer

Palm Pals is a real plush brand with low prices, free shipping over $35 and 30-day returns on unopened items. We couldn't find a Trustpilot page or ratings on the page we opened, so there's little public feedback to weigh.

What Palm Pals sells

Small plush characters in themed collections such as Barn, Celestial, Forest, Mythical, Pets, Galactic and Sports, plus licensed Care Bears. Each character has a little story and an ID number.

When we checked, Care Bears were $12.79 each, Clover Bunny was $11.99 and gift cards ran from $10 to $100.

Is Palm Pals legit?

It looks legitimate. It has its own store, written shipping and return policies, and sells a licensed Care Bears line that its pages say is by Aurora. Google searches also pair the two names.

Is it worth the money?
