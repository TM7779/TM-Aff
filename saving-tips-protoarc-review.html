The short answer

ProtoArc is a real US business based in Houston, Texas, that sells through its own store and through Amazon and Walmart. Prices are moderate, most products were on sale when we checked, and a 30-day money-back trial lets you try a mouse or chair at home. Independent reviews of the store are few.

What ProtoArc sells

Ergonomic mice (vertical, trackball and compact), foldable, split and backlit keyboards, keyboard and mouse combos, trackpads, ergonomic office chairs and seat cushions.

Prices run from $17.99 for the EM15 compact mouse to $229.99 for the EC300 chair, on sale.

Is ProtoArc legit?

Yes. Its store is registered in Houston, Texas, it lists a support email and phone number, and its written return policy gives 30 days for a full refund, followed by a warranty. Some orders ship through Amazon's fulfillment network.

Is it worth the money?

A vertical mouse at $26.99 or a foldable keyboard at $43.99 is a low-cost way to try an ergonomic setup, and the 30-day trial takes most of the risk out. The chairs cost more, so check the size and return terms before you order.
