1. Pick the smallest tool that does the job

CarPal (€40.99) is the entry tool: an all-system check-up and the 6 most common resets from your phone. The TopScan comes in three editions, Lite (€65.99), Pro (€109.00) and Master (€175.99), with bi-directional tests that send commands to parts such as injectors and windows. Check your car's make, model and year against the tool's coverage on its product page before you buy. Most of the angry reviews of TOPDON come from tools that didn't support a car or a function.

2. Check what software updates cost

Some TOPDON scanners sell software updates separately once the included period ends: the store lists a one-year update for the ArtiDiag800 at €115.00 and for the ArtiDiag100 at €109.00 (without VAT). Software and subscriptions can't be returned, so read what's included before you pay.

3. Decide within 14 days if you might return it

TOPDON's return fee depends on timing. An unopened tool returned within 14 days has no service fee; between day 15 and day 30 the fee is 10%. An opened but unused tool costs 10% within 14 days and 15% after, and a used one 20%. You also pay the return shipping, so try the tool on your car soon after it arrives.

Shipping and returns

TOPDON Europe ships to the EU, the UK, Switzerland, Norway, Turkey and a few other European countries. EU orders go by DHL; most UK orders go by Royal Mail. Orders are usually processed in 3 to 7 business days, and the shipping cost, based on weight and destination, shows at checkout. Customs fees outside the EU are paid by the buyer.

Returns are accepted within 30 days, in the original packaging with all accessories, minus the service fees above. Refunds go to the original payment method, usually within 2 to 3 business days of the return arriving. Faulty products are repaired or replaced under warranty.
