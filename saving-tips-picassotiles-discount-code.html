Shipping and returns

PicassoTiles ships Monday to Friday to the 48 contiguous US states, within 3 to 15 business days of the order. It doesn't ship to Alaska, Hawaii, Puerto Rico, other US territories or military addresses. Express 2-business-day shipping shows at checkout when the order qualifies, for an extra charge. The address can't be changed and the order can't be cancelled once it's placed.

Returns and exchanges are accepted within 30 days of receipt for sets that are unopened, in the complete original box. Shipping costs aren't refunded, and you pay the return shipping. For a broken or defective set, email the store's support address with proof of purchase for a replacement.
