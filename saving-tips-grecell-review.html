The short answer

GRECELL is a real power station brand with low prices, a 24-month warranty and a 30-day money-back guarantee. Its Trustpilot score is 3.8, and the bad reviews are serious: units that failed and slow replies from support.

What GRECELL sells

Portable power stations from 240W to 4000W, solar panels, motorcycle batteries and EV chargers.

When we checked, the 240W was $99.99, the T300S $149.99, the T-1000 $359.99 and the 4000W started at $1,599.99. Battery types differ: the 240W uses LiFePO4, while the T300S and T-1000 use NCM lithium-ion.
