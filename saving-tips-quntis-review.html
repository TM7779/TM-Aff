The short answer

Quntis is a real desk lighting brand with a very high store rating and a more mixed 3.9 Trustpilot score. It offers 30-day returns and free shipping in six countries, and some buyers report bars failing after months.

What Quntis sells

Monitor light bars for flat and curved screens, foldable desk lamps, outdoor solar lights, fairy lights and neon signs.

When we checked, monitor light bars ran from $57.99 for the Glow to $75.99 for the Focus Radar. Quntis markets several models as eye care; we haven't tested them and make no claim about that.

Positive Trustpilot reviews praise delivery, communication and product quality. The one-star reviews mention light bars that stopped working after days or months, orders not received after several weeks, and support that was hard to reach. Quntis has claimed its Trustpilot page.

Is Quntis legit?

Yes. Quntis says it has served more than 2 million users since 2019, publishes shipping and return policies, and gives a return address in Ontario, California.

Is it worth the money?

If you work at a desk and want light on the desk rather than on the screen, the bars cost about $70, and the 30-day return window gives you time to try one. Keep your order details, because the complaints we read are about bars failing later on.
