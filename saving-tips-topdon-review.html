The short answer

TOPDON is a real company, founded in 2017, with a European arm in Barcelona. Its tools are cheaper than many professional scanners, but its Trustpilot scores are low: 2.0 out of 5 from 20 reviews for topdon.com and 1.9 from 14 for topdon.us. Most complaints are about a tool not supporting a car or a function, updates and logins, and slow support. Check coverage before you buy.

What TOPDON sells

OBD2 and diagnostic scanners, from phone-based dongles (CarPal, TopScan) to handheld and tablet tools (the ArtiDiag and Phoenix lines); battery testers and chargers; jump starters; thermal cameras for phones; and EV chargers.

When we checked, the European store's best sellers were the TopScan (from €65.99), the CarPal (€40.99) and the ArtiDiag600 S (€220.83), all without VAT.

Is TOPDON legit?

Yes. TOPDON was founded in 2017, and TOPDON EUROPE SL lists a street address in Barcelona, phone numbers for the EU and the UK, and support at support.eu@topdon.com. The European store publishes shipping, return and warranty policies, and the brand sells through Amazon and other retailers too. The low ratings are about products and service, not about orders that never arrived.

Is it worth the money?

For a DIY driver who wants to read fault codes and do common resets from a phone, the CarPal or a TopScan costs less than most handheld scanners. The risk is a tool that can't do the job on your car, and returns cost money: a 10% to 20% service fee for an opened tool, plus return shipping. Check the coverage list for your car first, and look at what software updates cost after the included period.
