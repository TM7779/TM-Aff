1. Pick the screen size you need

Most Carpuride motorcycle screens come in a 5-inch and a 7-inch version, and the smaller one costs less. The W502 Pro is $319.99 against $339.99 for the 7-inch W702 Pro, and the 5-inch W502BS for BMW bikes is $409.90 against $429.90 for the W702BS. That's $20 saved before the code.

Tire pressure sensors (TPMS) are an add-on on several models, usually $20 more. Skip them if your bike already shows tire pressure.

2. Only pay for the cameras you'll use

For cars, the W125A ($299.90) comes with a 4K dash cam and a 1080P backup camera. The W125S ($319.90) adds a 1080P infrared cabin camera that Carpuride describes for watching the back seat at night. If you don't need the cabin camera, the W125A saves $20.

If you only want CarPlay or Android Auto on a dashboard screen, the C3 ($159.99, with a backup camera) and the C92 ($169.99) cost about half as much.

3. Check compatibility before you order

Every product page carries the same warning: the screens don't work with HarmonyOS phones or some Android models, and there's a compatibility list linked from each page. The BS models are made for BMW motorcycles with the Wonder Wheel controller and have their own BMW list. A return for a wrong purchase costs you the return shipping (see below), so checking first is the cheapest saving.

Shipping and returns

Shipping is free on all products. Carpuride says orders ship within 1 to 3 days by DHL, UPS or DPD and usually arrive in 7 to 15 business days, or within about 2 days from its local warehouses in some areas. The tracking number is emailed after dispatch.

Returns are accepted within 30 days of receiving the package, but you must email Carpuride's customer service first for a return authorization; returns sent without one are refused. Items must be complete, with packaging, manuals and accessories. Opened or used items without a quality problem may be refused. If you simply changed your mind, you pay the return shipping; if the item is faulty, Carpuride pays. The product pages also list a 2-year warranty.

If the Carpuride code doesn't work
