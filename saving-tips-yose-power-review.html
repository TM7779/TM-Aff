The short answer

Yes, Yose Power is a real company that has sold e-bike batteries and kits since 2011, by its own account, with a warehouse in Berlin and an EU hub in Frankfurt. It holds 4.3 out of 5 on Trustpilot from 177 reviews. Most buyers are happy with the kits and the delivery, but about one review in four is 1 star, mostly about faulty batteries or chargers and slow email support.

What Yose Power sells

Conversion kits turn a normal bike into an e-bike: a wheel with a hub motor built in, a controller, a display, a pedal sensor and, in most kits, a battery and charger. Yose Power sells front and rear kits for 20" to 28" wheels, from €219 for a 20" front kit without a battery to €459 for the A3 Pro rear kit with a torque sensor. It also sells replacement 36V batteries for down tubes and rear racks (from €186) and spare parts like displays, controllers, brake sensors and pedal sensors.

Happy reviewers mention fast delivery, kits that are easy to fit, tools in the box, and support that answers technical questions within a day or two. Several are on their second or third kit, and one says a kit lasted 23,000 km. The 1-star reviews are worth reading before you buy: batteries that wouldn't charge or never reached full charge, a faulty replacement charger and a warranty dispute, and complaints that support is by email only and slow. One buyer was unhappy that no kit fit their bike.

Is Yose Power legit?

Yes. It sells through its own site as well as Amazon and eBay, says it has run its own ISO 9001 certified factory since 2017, and lists warehouses in Germany, the UK and the US. It publishes its shipping, returns, warranty and repair prices, ships every parcel with tracking, and accepts PayPal, cards and Klarna. Its contact is email only (service@yosepower.com, Monday to Saturday), with no phone number, which some reviewers dislike.

Is it worth the money?

A kit with a battery costs €379 to €459, a fraction of a new e-bike, if you're happy to fit it yourself or pay a bike shop. The battery prices are also fair for the capacity: the 20Ah Hailong pack gives 740Wh for €259. The risk is after-sales: if a battery fails, you deal with Yose Power by email, and you pay shipping on warranty parts. Check that your local e-bike rules allow the motor power and top speed you pick; the 250W kits list 25 km/h, the 350W kits 30 to 32 km/h. Who it's for

Yose Power suits riders in Europe who want to turn a bike they already own into an e-bike, or replace a worn 36V battery, and who are comfortable checking wheel sizes, dropout widths and connectors. If you want a ready-built e-bike with a local shop to call, buy one from a dealer instead.
