The short answer

Bird&Be is a legitimate North American brand with a highly rated prenatal and convenient daily packs. It costs more than a drugstore prenatal; it's worth it if you want everything in one pack each day.

What Bird&Be sells

Doctor-formulated prenatal packs (Complete, Gentle), fertility packs for women and men, postpartum packs, and at-home sperm, ovulation and pregnancy tests.

The Complete Prenatal Pack is $45 for 30 days, about $1.50 a day.
