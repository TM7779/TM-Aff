Shipping and returns

Tidradio says it ships within 1 to 3 days and that shipping takes 4 to 5 business days on average, depending on location. You have 12 days from delivery to ask for an exchange or refund. Items must be new with all original packaging, and you pay the return shipping. In the US, transmitting on ham or GMRS frequencies generally needs an FCC license, so check the rules where you live.
