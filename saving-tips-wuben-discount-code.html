1. Buy a bundle instead of two lights

The L3 comes in bundles with a second light, such as the L3 + G5 for $58.99, against $39.99 + $18.39 = $58.38 bought apart. That bundle saves nothing at the prices we saw, so add up the two lights before choosing a bundle.

2. Pick a keychain light if you need a small one

The E2 PULSE weighs 10.7 g and was $19.97, the G5 (400 lumens) $18.39. With the code they come to $17.97 and $16.55. The bigger L3 and X1 Pro cost two to six times as much.

3. Register your light for the 5-year warranty

It doesn't lower the price, but it protects it: Wuben gives a 15-day replacement for quality problems, a 1-year warranty on flashlights and rechargeable batteries, and free repairs for 5 years to people who register the product on its site (accessories and batteries excluded). You pay shipping for repairs.

Shipping and returns

Orders take 2 business days to prepare. To the US, Canada and Mexico, delivery takes 3 to 7 business days from an overseas warehouse, or 12 to 28 business days when it ships from China. Wuben doesn't ship to some countries, including Saudi Arabia, the UAE, Qatar, Turkey and Pakistan.

Returns must be requested within 30 days of delivery, unused and in the original packaging, by emailing support@wubenlight.com. You pay return shipping unless the item is defective. Report missing or damaged items within 1 week of delivery.

On Trustpilot, 277 of the 423 reviews give 5 stars and 103 give 1 star. Fans praise bright, compact lights such as the E2, E7, G5 and X4, and helpful support.

The 1-star reviews mostly describe shipping: orders that took weeks, incomplete tracking, or lights that never arrived, with one reviewer calling it a dropshipping service. A few mention build issues, such as a mode selector that broke. The store's own ratings are higher than Trustpilot's, as is common.

Is Wuben legit?

Yes, by what we could check. Its Trustpilot profile lists an address in Palatine, Illinois, and the store's account lists Mong Kok, Hong Kong. It publishes its shipping, return and warranty policies, and ships from warehouses in the US and Germany as well as China, which explains why delivery times vary so much.

Is Wuben worth the money?

For small lights, yes: an E2 PULSE or G5 costs under $20 on sale, and the 5-year repair warranty for registered products is longer than many budget brands offer. If you need the light by a set date, check at checkout whether it ships from an overseas warehouse (3 to 7 business days to North America) or from China (12 to 28).
