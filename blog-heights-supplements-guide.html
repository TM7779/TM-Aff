Vitals⁺: the everyday multivitamin

Vitals⁺ is where most people start. Two capsules give you 20 nutrients, including omega-3 (EPA and DHA), the full B complex and vitamin D. That covers the separate fish oil, B vitamins and vitamin D many people already take.

  • What's inside: vitamins A, C, D and E, the full B complex, zinc, selenium, iron and omega-3, in delayed-release capsules.
  • How to take it: 2 capsules a day with water or juice, at any time, with or without food. Heights suggests first thing so you don't forget.
  • Good to know: vitamin B12 and iron contribute to the reduction of tiredness and fatigue, and vitamins C and D and zinc contribute to normal immune function. Heights recommends taking it for at least three months.

Biotic⁺: a one-a-day probiotic

Biotic⁺ combines seven live bacteria strains with zinc, at 20 billion CFU in a single capsule. The capsule is delayed-release, so the bacteria reach your gut instead of breaking down in your stomach, and it doesn't need to be kept in the fridge.

  • How to take it: 1 capsule a day, at any time. Heights suggests the morning. Don't take more than one.
  • Good to know: zinc contributes to normal immune function. It pairs with Vitals⁺ in the Daily Essentials Duo.

Magnesium⁺: one ingredient, done properly

Magnesium⁺ contains a single ingredient: magnesium glycinate, a form known for being gentle on the stomach. Two capsules give you 300mg of magnesium, about 80% of the EU daily reference intake of 375mg.

  • How to take it: 2 capsules a day. Heights suggests the evening, but any regular time works.
  • Good to know: magnesium contributes to the reduction of tiredness and fatigue and to normal muscle function. It's also the cheapest way into the range.

Thrive⁺: for healthy ageing

Thrive⁺ is the newest product in the range and the most premium. One capsule combines four ingredients, including Niagen® (a form of vitamin B3 the body uses to make NAD⁺), CoQ10 and trans-resveratrol. Heights designed it for cellular energy and healthy ageing.

  • How to take it: 1 capsule in the morning, ideally with some healthy fat. Heights suggests taking it alongside the omega-3 in Vitals⁺.
  • Good to know: it's vegan, and it pairs with Vitals⁺ in the Longevity Duo.

Hydrate⁺: electrolytes with a focus twist

Hydrate⁺ is an electrolyte mix with added Cognizin® citicoline. It comes in three flavours (Lime Margarita, Peach Spritz and Mango Ginger Mule), and a starter pack lets you try all three before you commit.

  • Good for: hot days, workouts, travel and long working days.
